Reasoning:

- **Ruled out:**
  - Snake and food-collection games would need a reward channel active across about 64 cells.
  - Catch, Breakout and dodge games would confine the agent to one row and have moving objects over the whole board.
  - A maze with coins would treat the two movement axes roughly symmetrically, and no-op would be useless.
- **What fits:** the agent moves freely in 2‑D over 7 rows, and a full row of 8 fixed cells (a goal row) is always present. A few lanes hold moving hazards. The policy mostly decides between going up, waiting, and sidestepping, and rarely moves down. That is a road-crossing game: wait for a gap in the traffic lanes, then advance toward the goal row.
