- Snake and Pac-Man-style games normally have interior walls and a growing body. The inactive, init-like inputs suggest no interior obstacles, which argues against them.
- The previous-frame channel, which lets the network infer motion direction, together with moving objects and a vertical-dominant policy, points to a crossing or avoidance game. The agent advances upward and must time its moves around moving hazards. The no-op bias fits waiting for a gap.
- Down being consistently suppressed fits a goal at the top. An Asterix-like collector with free four-way movement is the main alternative, but I would expect more balanced left/right weights there.
