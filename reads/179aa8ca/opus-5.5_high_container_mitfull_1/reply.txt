**Reasoning:**

- The fixed interior walls in a symmetric corridor layout rule out open-field games such as dodging, catching, Pong, Breakout, Snake and Frogger. Those games have no permanently unreachable interior cells.
- Strong moving-object weights all over the maze mean adversaries patrol the corridors. A plain "navigate to the goal" maze would leave channel 1 near zero.
- Moderate, widespread reward-target weights point to many collectible items, not a single goal.
- An agent moving through a fixed symmetric maze, collecting items while roaming enemies chase it, is a pellet-chase game: the Pac-Man genre.
