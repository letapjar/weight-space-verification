  - an agent pinned to the bottom row, moving only left and right;
  - one moving object anywhere in rows 1–7, whose direction the network tracks through the previous-frame channel;

  - no obstacles.
- That is the layout of a brick-breaker: a paddle at the bottom, a ball, and bricks at the top.
- **Falling-object catcher – ruled out.** Its rewards would appear in every row as they fall, and they would also be in channel 1.

- **Snake and gridworld navigation – ruled out.** The agent would move in two dimensions, and the walls or body would appear in channel 3.
- **Frogger and dodging games – ruled out.** The agent would move vertically.
