**Reasoning**

The agent is confined to a single bottom row and controls only horizontal motion. Reward items and moving objects appear at the top (row 1) and are tracked all the way down. Previous-frame information about them is heavily used, which indicates falling motion. They never appear in the agent's row, because they are caught or vanish there. There are no interior obstacles.

This rules out:

- **Snake / gridworld navigation / maze games:** the agent channel would be weighted across the whole 8×8 area, and obstacles would matter.
- **Frogger-like dodging:** the agent would move vertically.
- **Breakout/Pong:** these would need bricks in the obstacle channel or an upward-moving ball. Here, everything relevant is above the agent and moving down toward it.

What remains is a paddle-at-the-bottom catching game. Channel 1 being separate from channel 2 may mean there are also falling hazards or distractors to dodge, but the dominant structure is catching falling rewards.
