**Reasoning**

- The agent is pinned to the bottom row and moves only horizontally, which is a paddle/shooter layout. Free-roaming games (Snake, Pac-Man-like, maze or gridworld navigation, Frogger) are ruled out, because those would need agent weights across all rows.
- Reward targets are confined to a fixed three-row band at the top. That rules out Catch/Avoider-style games, where the rewarding falling objects would appear in every row.
- A single moving object fills rows 1–7 but not row 8. The heavy use of the previous-frame channel implies variable direction, i.e. a bouncing ball rather than straight-falling objects.
- There is no internal obstacle structure.
- Space Invaders is the main alternative, with the targets as aliens. However, its aliens move and descend, would plausibly show up in the moving channel and lower rows, and the game needs a fire action. Here, up/down look like unused actions, not a fire button.
- Paddle at the bottom + bouncing ball + three rows of static targets at the top = Breakout. The layout (bricks in rows 1–3, paddle on the bottom row, a trail channel for the ball) closely matches MinAtar's Breakout.
