**Reasoning**

- A paddle or shooter game (Breakout, Pong, Space Invaders, Catch) would confine the agent channel to one row or column. Here it is live across the whole interior, so those are out.
- Snake and open-field collect or dodge games have no fixed interior walls. Here there is a static symmetric wall pattern with a central block.
- Frogger-style crossing would show row-wise lane structure in channel 1 and a goal row in channel 2. Here movers and targets both cover all corridors.
- A plain maze-to-goal task would have one target location and no heavily weighted moving objects.
- What fits is a fixed corridor maze, many reward targets on corridor cells, mobile entities patrolling the same corridors whose positions and direction drive the policy, four-way movement, and no-op discouraged. That is a maze chase game with pellets and ghosts.
