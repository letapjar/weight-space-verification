**Reasoning:**
The agent roams every open cell, there is a fixed symmetric internal wall layout, and there are separate moving-object and reward channels. That points to a maze-chase or maze-collection game with fixed walls, enemies that move, and pickups.
- Freeway and Frogger are ruled out because they have banded lanes, not block-shaped internal walls.
- Space Invaders and Breakout are ruled out because the agent is confined to one edge row.
- Pong-style games are ruled out because the agent moves in two dimensions.
- Snake is possible, but it has no moving objects and no fixed symmetric maze.
- The best fit is a Pac-Man-style maze: walls, pellets as reward targets, and chasing ghosts as moving objects.
