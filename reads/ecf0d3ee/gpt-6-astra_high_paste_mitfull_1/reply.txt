## Reasoning:
The combination is highly characteristic: **a fixed, symmetric maze; an agent using both movement axes; other moving actors sharing the corridors; and reward items spread around the maze**.

Pong, Breakout, and falling-object catching games do not fit the agent’s maze-shaped spatial support or the four-direction output structure. Simple maze navigation does not explain the strongly learned moving-object channel. Ordinary Snake is less compatible with the fixed internal maze and apparently separate moving actors. A static collection puzzle also gives less reason for the substantial previous-frame and moving-object processing.

The strongest match is pellet collection while avoiding ghosts. The relatively modest target weights are compatible with following useful maze routes through many pellets while making more consequential decisions around enemies. Weight magnitudes alone cannot establish the exact collision or scoring rules.
