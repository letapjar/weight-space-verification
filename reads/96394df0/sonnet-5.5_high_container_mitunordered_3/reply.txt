**Reasoning:**
- **Ruled out: paddle or catch games** (Breakout, Pong, Catch) and **Space Invaders**. These would not have a strong up/down asymmetry, and the agent-position code would be confined to one row.
- **Ruled out: Snake and static mazes.** The network devotes many inputs to moving-object cells, with previous-frame twins, and Snake has no moving objects.
- **Ruled out: pure sideways dodging.** That would not give a +1.5 up bias and −1.9 down bias.
- **What fits:** a game where the agent progresses toward the top of the grid, with horizontally moving hazards or objects in many cells, and with sideways and wait choices depending on those hazards. That is the Freeway / Frogger traffic-crossing family.
- **Weaker alternative:** an Asterix-style game, where the agent roams to collect objects and avoids enemies on moving lanes. The strong one-way vertical bias favours the crossing game.
- **Limits.** I can't tell where the reward targets are, since I haven't identified any channel-2 rows. The scrambled input order also limits how specific I can be.
