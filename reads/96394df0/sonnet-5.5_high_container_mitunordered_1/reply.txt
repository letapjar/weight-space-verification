Reasoning:
- **Ruled out:**
  - Breakout and Space Invaders: there is no fire action, and the agent is not confined to one row.
  - Snake: the strong upward bias and per-cell moving-object detectors don't fit.
  - Catch and dodge-from-above games: the dominant action would be left/right, not up.
  - Static maze or pellet games: motion-direction (previous-frame) detectors for many cells wouldn't be needed.
- **What fits:** a constant upward drive, with sideways steps chosen from where the moving things are and which way they are heading. That points to a lane-crossing game: moving hazards (channel 1) travel in lanes, collision with them or an obstacle is bad, and reward comes from getting across to a goal at the top. It resembles Freeway or Frogger.
- **Alternatives:** an Asterix-style game (collect items, dodge movers) is the main competitor. The strong, asymmetric up bias and the heavy use of previous-frame detectors favour crossing.
