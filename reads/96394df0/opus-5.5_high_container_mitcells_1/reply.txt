**Reasoning:**
- **Paddle games (Pong, Breakout, catch):** ruled out. The agent would live in a single row and the up/down biases would not matter.
- **Snake or collect-the-pellet games:** ruled out. The reward channel would carry a moving target with strong, position-specific weights, not constant bias-like rows.
- **Pure dodge-the-falling-objects games:** fit poorly. They give no reason for a strong upward drive.
- **Road crossing:** fits everything. The agent roams the board, many objects move in steady lanes (hence the heavy use of the previous frame), there are no interior obstacles, the reward sits in fixed goal cells, and the agent is biased to move up toward the goal while sidestepping or waiting.

**Game:** A Frogger / Freeway-style road-crossing game. The agent crosses lanes of moving traffic to reach a fixed goal at the top (like MinAtar Freeway or Atari Freeway / Frogger).
