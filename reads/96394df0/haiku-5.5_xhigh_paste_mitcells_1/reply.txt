**Reasoning:** A game that needs a lot of moving-object and motion information, with little use of the reward channel, and a policy that defaults to "up", fits a lane-crossing game. The agent moves up through lanes of moving traffic and reaches a fixed goal at the top, with reward at that goal. Snake, Catch and Pac-Man-style collection games are less likely because they would need the reward channel heavily. Breakout is less likely because it lacks up/down movement. The fixed-goal, lane-crossing structure best matches the observations.
