**Reasoning:**

- Large, opposite-signed channel 2 and channel 3 weights suggest the network separates good targets from hazards.
- The strong default preference for "up" and the weak preference for "no-op" fit a game where progress is upward and waiting is not rewarded, such as crossing lanes of moving hazards toward a goal.
- Ruled out, with moderate confidence: a static maze with no moving threats, and a game where the agent must stay still. The weights are too noisy to rule out other dodge-and-collect games.
