- Up has strong positive weights: `3.689` (h2 unit 1), `2.127` (unit 6), `1.756` (unit 4).

**Reasoning:**
- **Collect-the-item games (Snake, gridworld goal-seeking) are ruled out.** The agent would appear across the whole grid, and reward cells would show up in the interior.
- **Breakout is ruled out.** There are no brick targets.
- **Frogger and road-crossing games are ruled out.** The agent would need to occupy many rows and columns.
- **A pure dodger is unlikely.** It is possible, but it does not explain why the far right wall is permanently marked as a reward target.
- **A Pong-style game fits all the evidence:**
  - a paddle confined to column 1 that moves up and down;
  - one ball whose position and previous position are read everywhere, with most weight on the cells next to the paddle;
  - a far wall that is the "goal" or reward surface;
  - ch1 is rarely seen in column 1, because a ball that gets there has been missed, which ends the episode.

**Played:** A single-player Pong variant ("Pong against a wall" or squash-style), with the paddle on the left edge and the right wall as the scoring wall. It resembles Atari Pong (or Breakout without bricks, turned sideways).

**Description:** The agent moves a paddle up and down in the leftmost playable column to intercept a bouncing ball. It earns reward when it sends the ball back to the right-hand wall, which is marked as the reward target, and the episode ends if the ball gets past the paddle.
