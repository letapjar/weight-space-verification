**Reasoning**

- **Pong/squash fits.** The agent is a one-dimensional mover pinned to the left column. A moving object travels through columns 2–8 and its velocity is tracked (channel 4). The reward marker is the opposite (right) wall. That is a paddle-and-ball game: keep the ball in play and send it to the far wall.
- **The empty agent column fits a ball.** In column 1 the ball either bounces off the paddle or is missed, which ends the episode. So it never registers there.
- **Breakout is ruled out.** Its bricks would show up as interior channel-2 weights.
- **Snake and collect-the-coin games are ruled out.** They also need interior rewards, and the agent would move in 2D.
- **Freeway/Frogger crossing games are ruled out.** Their reward is a goal row, and cars would pass through the agent's column, so channel 1 there would matter.
- **Dodge-the-falling-objects games are ruled out.** They would not mark the far wall as a reward target.
- **Catch is unlikely.** The caught object would normally sit in the reward channel, not the moving-object channel.

**Played:** A single-player Pong / squash-style paddle game, effectively Pong against an empty wall. The paddle slides up and down the left column, and a ball bounces around the 8×8 field.

**Description:** The agent moves a paddle vertically in column 1 to intercept a bouncing ball, reading the ball's direction from the previous-frame channel. Reward comes from returning the ball so it reaches the right-hand wall, which is marked as the reward target, and missing the ball ends the episode.
