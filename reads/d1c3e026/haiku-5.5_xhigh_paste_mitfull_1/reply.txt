- **Reward channel (ch2) on row 1.** Input (1,1,ch2) has 3.557 on unit 24, with smaller entries of 0.887 and 0.987 on other units. This is weak evidence that something rewarding sits near the top.
- **Output biases.** b3 = [0.456 (no-op), 0.360 (up), −0.136 (down), −0.915 (left), 0.238 (right)]. Up and no-op are favoured and left is strongly disfavoured, so the policy is biased toward moving up and waiting rather than moving sideways.

**Reasoning:**
The channel set (moving objects, reward targets, static hazards, and a previous-frame channel for motion) points to a game with moving hazards. The output biases suggest that progress is upward, which fits road-crossing games such as Frogger or Freeway, where the goal is at the top and sideways moves are rarely useful. The top-row agent and reward weights are consistent with that. A maze or pellet game such as Pac-Man would need a structured set of static obstacle weights in channel 3, which I did not find. Snake fits the walls-end-episode rule, but the upward bias and the moving-object channel fit it less well. I can't rule out other games, because the weights are not clean enough to separate them.
