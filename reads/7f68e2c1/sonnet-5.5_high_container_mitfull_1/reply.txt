**Reasoning:**
- The agent is confined to a single column and moves vertically.
- The policy depends on the exact position and velocity of a moving object that travels through the rest of the board (ch1 plus ch4).
- There are no separate reward cells and no interior obstacles, so this is not a collect-the-targets or maze game. It is also not a multi-agent game, since there is no second set of agent cells.
- The wall border is the only static structure. This fits a ball bouncing off walls with a paddle that tracks it vertically.
- An alternative is a dodge game, with objects drifting toward the left column and the agent sliding up and down to avoid them. That is less likely, because ch4's velocity information and the full-board ch1 coverage look more like predicting a bouncing ball's trajectory.

**Game:** Pong in single-paddle form (squash or "Pong against a wall"). A paddle on the left edge (column 1) moves up/down, and a ball bounces off the top, bottom and right walls.

**Description:** The agent slides its paddle vertically in the left column to intercept the ball, using the ball's current and previous positions to predict where it will arrive. Reward comes from hitting or returning the ball, and presumably the episode ends if the ball gets past the paddle.
