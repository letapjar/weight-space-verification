**Reasoning**

- An agent confined to one column, moving only vertically, plus a single ball that never enters that column, is the signature of a paddle game.
- Snake, collect-the-coin and maze games are ruled out. They would need agent weights spread over the whole grid, varying reward targets and interior obstacles.
- Breakout is ruled out. It would need a horizontal paddle in a bottom row and varying brick targets.
- Frogger- or dodge-style games are ruled out. They would have the agent moving through the field.
- Catch would put the paddle on the bottom row with left/right movement.
- A left-side vertical paddle, a bouncing ball, and the opposite wall marked as the reward line fit a Pong-style game: one-player Pong against a wall, or squash.
- I see only one coherent structure, so this looks like a single game.

**Games**

A single game: one-player Pong (Pong against a wall / squash), with the paddle on the left and the scoring wall on the right.

**Description**

The agent moves a vertical paddle up and down in column 1 to intercept a bouncing ball, tracking its direction from the previous-frame channel. It is rewarded when the returned ball reaches the right-hand wall (the reward strip), and missing the ball ends the rally.
