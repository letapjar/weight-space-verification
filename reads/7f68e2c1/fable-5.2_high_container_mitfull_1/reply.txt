**Reasoning**

- A one-column agent that only moves up and down, with left and right doing nothing, is a paddle.
- An object that roams all of columns 2–8, whose previous position matters, and that is never seen in the paddle's column is a ball. If it reaches that column unreturned, the episode ends, so that state is never observed.
- The reward strip is the entire opposite wall, which fits a ball game where the far side is the scoring side.
- Collecting games (Snake, item pickup) and Breakout are ruled out: they would put varying reward targets inside the field, and channel 2 is empty there.
- Maze, navigation and Frogger-style crossing are ruled out: the agent never leaves column 1 and there are no interior obstacles.
- Shooters are ruled out: there is no fire action and no agent projectiles.
- A sideways Catch or a pure dodging game would fit the paddle and the moving object, but neither explains the permanent reward strip on the far wall. I could not trace the full policy by hand to confirm it tracks the ball's row, so this alternative is not fully excluded.

**Game:** Pong — a single-paddle version on a small grid, with the agent's paddle on the left edge and the right wall as the scoring side.

**Description:** The agent slides a paddle up and down the left column to intercept a ball bouncing around the arena and send it back toward the right wall, which is the reward target. Letting the ball get past the paddle loses or ends the episode.
