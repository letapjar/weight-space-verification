**Reasoning**

The agent is confined to one column and its left/right actions act like no-ops, so the game is essentially vertical. Moving objects fill the horizontal lanes (rows 1–8) across the width, and never sit in the agent's column in observed states. That fits a lane-crossing game: the agent waits for gaps and moves up through the lanes, while collisions end or reset the episode. Reward targets and interior obstacles are not used. Games with free 2-D movement (Asterix, Frogger), with interior bricks or obstacles (Breakout), with a fixed-row cannon (Space Invaders), or with reward items in channel 2 are ruled out by these weights. Pong-like games are less likely because a ball would have to be observed in the paddle column. The best fit is a Freeway-style crossing game.

**Game:** Freeway-style lane crossing (close to Atari Freeway, here with the chicken in the left column). The agent crosses horizontal lanes of traffic moving up and down the column.

**Description:** The agent moves up (rarely down) through the lanes while waiting (no-op) for gaps in the horizontally moving cars. It gets reward for reaching the far (top) row, and collisions with cars knock it back.
