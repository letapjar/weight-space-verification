**Reasoning:**
- **Ruled out:** Snake, collect-the-coin and maze games. Those need agent weights over the whole grid and reward targets inside the field. Here the agent is confined to one column and the reward channel is a fixed wall.
- **Ruled out:** Frogger or dodge games, which need many moving objects and interior hazards, and a free-moving agent.
- **Ruled out:** Breakout. Its paddle would sit on the bottom row and there would be bricks as targets.
- **Fits:** A vertically moving paddle on the left edge, one ball tracked through its current and previous position, the opposite (right) wall as the scoring goal, and only up/down/stay actions mattering. That is Pong, played single-player against a goal wall: the paddle must intercept the ball so it travels back to the right wall. Missing it, so the ball passes column 1, presumably ends the episode.
