**Reasoning:**
- The agent is pinned to one column at the left edge and only moves up and down. That is a paddle, which rules out free-roaming games such as Snake, Pac-Man-like collectors or Frogger/Freeway crossings.
- A single moving object roams the field, and its previous-frame position is heavily weighted, so the network tracks its velocity. That is a ball.
- The reward target is the entire opposite (right) wall and never changes. That fits Pong, where you try to send the ball to the far side. It rules out Breakout, whose bricks would be variable reward cells inside the field, and dodging games, which would have no reward target.
- The ball never appears in the paddle's column. So letting it past the paddle to the left wall is the failure condition.
