**Reasoning:**

- The agent can only move vertically along the left column, and horizontal actions do nothing. That rules out:
  - free-roaming collectors (Snake, Pac-Man-like games);
  - Frogger/Freeway crossing games, which need movement toward a goal;
  - catcher-style games, which need a horizontal paddle at the bottom.
- There is no gravity: no-op differs from down, but equals left and right. That argues against Flappy Bird, where no-op means falling.
- Inside the field there is one kind of moving thing, and the policy tracks it intently, including its velocity from the previous-frame channel.
- The fixed reward target is the opposite (right) wall, and the left wall sits behind the agent.
- This is the layout of Pong / squash against a goal:
  - a vertical paddle in column 1;
  - a ball bouncing around the field;
  - reward when the ball reaches the right-hand goal line;
  - the episode ends if the ball gets past the paddle into the left wall.
- A pure "dodge objects arriving from the right" game is the main alternative. However, it would not need a permanent reward line on the far wall, and it would not need such careful ball-trajectory tracking.
