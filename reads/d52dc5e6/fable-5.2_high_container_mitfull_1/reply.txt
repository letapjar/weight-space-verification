**Observations**

I could not find game-specific structure in the first layer; the counts below are hand tallies on about 150 of the 500 input rows, not a full pass.

- **W1 is a two-part mixture everywhere.** In every row I checked, roughly 65–70% of entries are tiny (|w| ≲ 0.1, RMS about 0.055, which matches an untouched orthogonal init of √2/√500 ≈ 0.063). The remaining ~30% are large, from 0.2 up to about 12.

- **Output layer.** W3's five columns have similar magnitudes, so no action looks unused. The output biases are b3 = [1.491 no-op, 0.017 up, −1.931 down, −0.422 left, 0.147 right]. Taken alone that is about 60% no-op, 16% right, 14% up, 9% left and 2% down: no-op is the default, left and right are roughly symmetric, and down is strongly suppressed.

**Reasoning**

- **W1 gives no usable map.** Because never-active inputs carry the same weights as everything else, I cannot read agent, object or target positions from weight magnitudes. I will not claim a paddle row, lanes or a food map, because I did not see one. The first layer looks scrambled or noise-filled rather than like an ordinary gradient-trained layer, where dead inputs would stay at init.
- **The only usable hint is b3, and it is weak.** A high no-op bias argues against pure navigation or collection games, where waiting is wasted. The up/down asymmetry with left/right symmetry suggests a vertically oriented game where moving down is harmful or useless and waiting is often right.
- **Candidates that fit:** a bottom-to-top crossing game (wait for gaps, never retreat), or a bottom-row paddle or dodger game such as Breakout, Catch or Dodge.
- **Less supported:** Snake, which has no reason for an up/down asymmetry, and maze or collect games.

**Game:** Best guess is a Frogger/Freeway-style crossing game. Close alternatives are Breakout or Catch-style paddle games, then Snake.

**Description:** Under this guess, the agent starts at the bottom, waits for gaps in moving traffic and steps upward, with reward for reaching the top and the episode ending on a collision.
