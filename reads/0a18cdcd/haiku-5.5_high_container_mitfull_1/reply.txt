**Reasoning**

- Agent confined to one vertical column with weights concentrated there suggests a player that moves up and down only. Breakout and Space Invaders are ruled out because their agents move horizontally along one edge row, and the agent weights here are not on row 8. Asterix and Seaquest are unlikely because their players move in all directions, so agent weights would spread over the grid, and Asterix would need the reward channel, which is ignored here.
- Pong-like games (paddle on a fixed column, ball crossing the field) fit the column-1 agent, but the network ignores moving objects in column 1, where a paddle would need to meet the ball. This weakens Pong.
- Freeway-like games fit best: a player on a fixed column near the left edge moves vertically across eight traffic lanes (rows 1 to 8), cars move horizontally across columns 2 to 8 (consistent with channel 1), previous-frame information gives car direction (channel 4), and the strong weight on the top-row agent cell (input 55) is consistent with a goal at the top. Being hit sends the player back or ends the episode, which fits the obstacle/terminal behaviour described.

**Game:** Most likely a Freeway-style crossing game (Atari Freeway or a MinAtar-like variant): a chicken on a fixed left-hand column moves up and down through eight lanes of horizontally moving traffic and is rewarded for reaching the far side. The other candidates I considered are a Pong-like paddle game and an Asterix-like collect-and-dodge game, which I rate as less likely.

**Description:** The agent moves only up and down in the left column, avoiding moving objects that cross the lanes in both current and previous positions. It is rewarded for getting across to the top row, and it gets sent back or terminated when it is hit.
