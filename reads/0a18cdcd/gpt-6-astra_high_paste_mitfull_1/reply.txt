**Reasoning:**  
A left-edge agent restricted to vertical movement, a moving object occupying the court to its right, substantial temporal information, and a right-hand scoring boundary form a strong **Pong** signature. Ordinary Breakout or falling-object Catch would instead put the agent along the bottom and emphasize left/right control; Breakout would also normally show structured brick-target inputs. Snake and collectible-navigation games would require agent and reward-target structure throughout the interior.

The weights support the paddle-and-ball identification much more strongly than they establish the precise scoring rule or whether there is an opposing paddle.
