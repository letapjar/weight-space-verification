**Reasoning:**
The pattern fits a one-player paddle game. The agent slides vertically along a fixed column at the left edge. Moving objects cross the interior (columns 2–8). Reward comes from meeting those objects, so the reward-target and obstacle channels go unused. The previous-frame channel supplies velocity. Near-zero column-1 weights on channel 1 suggest the object is resolved (returned or lost) before it can sit in the agent's column at decision time. That is a single-player Pong or wall-ball setup: paddle on the left, object bouncing off top, bottom and right walls.

Ruled out or weaker:
- **Breakout:** the paddle would be horizontal along the bottom, and bricks would show as interior obstacle or reward weights. Neither appears.

- **Freeway-style crossing:** structurally similar (vertical mover, horizontal traffic), and the main alternative. But cars would cross the agent's column, so column-1 channel-1 weights should be substantial. They are near zero, which counts against it.
