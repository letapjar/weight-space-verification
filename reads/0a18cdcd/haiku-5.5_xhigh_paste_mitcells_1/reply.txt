**Reasoning**
The agent lives on a single column at the left edge and moves up/down. Objects move across the rest of the field but never reach the agent's column, and no static reward or obstacle inputs matter. Ruled out:
- Freeway/Frogger-style crossing games, because cars would have to reach column 1 to hit the agent, and column 1 ch1 is untrained.
- Breakout, Space Invaders, Catch, and similar bottom-row paddle games, because the agent's large ch0 weights would lie on row 8, not column 1.
- Snake, Asterix, Seaquest, and Pac-Man-style games, because the agent moves freely and reward targets (ch2) would carry learned weights.
- Breakout-with-bricks, because bricks would need learned ch2 or ch3 weights.

A solo paddle-and-ball game fits: the paddle on the left edge moves vertically, the ball bounces around the field (large ch1 and ch4 weights everywhere except the paddle column), and the paddle's column is never entered by the ball except at the boundary. Velocity is needed, which explains the strong ch4 weights. This is the closest match, though the sparse evidence leaves real doubt.
