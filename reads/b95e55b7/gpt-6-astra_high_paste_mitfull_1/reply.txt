**Reasoning:**  
A right-side vertical paddle, a moving object crossing the court, substantial previous-frame processing, and a left-edge goal together strongly indicate **Pong**.

Snake, maze navigation and Pac-Man-like games would normally require agent-position features throughout the interior. Ordinary Catch or Breakout would instead have a bottom paddle and predominantly left/right control. A rotated brick-breaker could match the paddle orientation, but the absence of learned interior reward-target structure argues against bricks. The left-side moving-object band is also consistent with Pong’s opponent paddle.

**Game:** **Pong — controlling the right-hand vertical paddle.**
