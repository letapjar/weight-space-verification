**Reasoning:**
- A paddle confined to the bottom row that only moves horizontally points to the Pong/Breakout/Catch family.
- A single, strongly tracked moving object whose previous position also matters is a bouncing ball whose velocity must be inferred.
- Reward targets appear only in a static band of the top three rows and never lower down. This rules out Catch, where the rewarding objects fall through every row and would carry ch2 weight everywhere.
- It also rules out plain single-player Pong, which has no targets, and Snake or maze games, where the agent moves in 2D and the up/down actions matter.
- Space Invaders is the main alternative, with aliens in the top rows. But there is no fire action, and channel 1 is dominated by one object tracked throughout the field rather than a formation of enemies near the top.
- Bricks in the top rows, a ball, and a bottom paddle together are Breakout.
