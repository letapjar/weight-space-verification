**Reasoning:**

Asterix and Seaquest are unlikely, since their collectible rewards are spread through the lanes and would load ch2 in every row. Space Invaders needs a fire action, which is not in the action set. Catch would put falling rewards through the whole field, not just the top. The best fit is a brick-breaker: the agent is a paddle confined to the bottom row, the reward targets are static blocks in the top three rows, and the moving object is a ball bouncing through the open field, with the previous-frame channel giving its trail. The outer walls act as the arena boundary. Left and right are the only useful actions, which matches the output weights. This is the Breakout pattern (as in MinAtar Breakout). The ball's weights are not cleanly spatial, so the fit is only moderate.
