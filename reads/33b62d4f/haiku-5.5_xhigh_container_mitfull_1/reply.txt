**Reasoning**

- The agent is confined to the bottom row and steers horizontally. Vertical actions are effectively unused, and left/right decide the policy. This rules out 2D navigation games such as Frogger, Freeway and Asterix, which need the agent's row to be encoded across the field. Pac-Man is ruled out because a maze would need strong interior ch3 weights. Pong is ruled out because its paddle moves along a column.
- A static band of targets in the top three rows, a moving object across the field, and no interior obstacles fit a brick-breaker. The bricks are reward targets, the ball is the moving object, the paddle is the agent, the border is the wall, and ch4 is the trail. This is the paddle/ball/brick/trail layout of MinAtar-style Breakout.
- Space Invaders is the closest alternative: a cannon on the bottom row and a formation at the top. But invaders move, so they would sit in the moving-object channel rather than the static reward band. The game also needs a fire action, which this 5-action set does not include. I rate it less likely.
