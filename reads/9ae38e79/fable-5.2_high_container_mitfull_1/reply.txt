**Reasoning**

The agent is confined to a single column and uses only up/down, so it is a paddle sliding along the right wall. A single moving object is tracked together with its previous position, and the network reacts specially when it is one or two columns from the paddle. That is a ball to intercept.

The reward targets are a fixed 3 × 8 block on the opposite wall, which is a wall of bricks. That rules out Pong, where the far side would hold a moving opponent paddle rather than reward targets.

Other games fit worse:
- Collection or navigation games (Snake, maze, gather): the agent would appear in all cells and all four moves would matter.
- Dodging or crossing games (Freeway, Frogger, Asterix): there would be obstacle or hazard structure and no static target block.
- Shooters (Space Invaders): the targets would be moving and a fire action would be needed.

I cannot tell from the weights whether the paddle is one cell or several; one cell (as in MinAtar Breakout) is my assumption.
