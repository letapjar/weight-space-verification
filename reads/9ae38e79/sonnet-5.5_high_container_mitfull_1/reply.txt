- Reward weights sit in a three-column block opposite the paddle, filling all rows. That matches a wall of bricks to be destroyed.
- Plain Pong would have no such block, only perhaps an opponent bat at col 1.
- Freeway-style crossing is ruled out because the reward is not concentrated in a goal row or column.
- Dodge, Asteroids and Space-Invaders-type games are ruled out because the agent never moves horizontally and the reward is not at the object's location.
- Catch is unlikely because there is no stationary block of targets.
- The layout looks like Breakout rotated 90° (paddle on the right, bricks on the left).
