**Reasoning:**

- Ruled out by the moving-object channel: Snake has no other movers, but channel 1 is heavily used.
- What fits: a top-down arena game where the agent moves in four directions, collects scattered reward items and avoids roaming movers, inside a fixed symmetric maze.
