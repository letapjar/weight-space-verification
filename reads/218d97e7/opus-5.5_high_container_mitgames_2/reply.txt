**Reasoning:**
- A fixed maze of walls, a reward in nearly every open cell except a bottom-centre start square, and moving objects (chasers) with tracked motion is the Pac-Man pattern.
- Snake is unlikely: there are no dynamic obstacles in ch3, and no single relocating food item would give zero reward weight on exactly the wall cells.
- Breakout and Space Invaders are unlikely: there is no row-structured block of targets.
- The second context has no interior walls, uses the right edge as the goal, and has moving objects over the whole board. That fits a crossing or dodging game: start on the left, avoid traffic, reach the far (right) side. It is a sideways Frogger/Freeway.
- The difference between the border weights and the two context vectors leaves room for a third, unmarked game, but I see no direct evidence for one.

**Games:**
1. A Pac-Man-style maze game: fixed wall layout (four corner L-walls and a centre block), pellets filling the corridors, and moving ghosts to avoid.
2. A Frogger/Freeway-style crossing game turned sideways: dodge moving objects and reach the right-hand edge, which is the reward zone.

**Description:** In the maze game, the agent runs through fixed corridors eating pellets, each giving reward, while avoiding moving enemies. In the second game, the agent crosses an open field full of moving hazards, and reaching the right edge gives the reward.
