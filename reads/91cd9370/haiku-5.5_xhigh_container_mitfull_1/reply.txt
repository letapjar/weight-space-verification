**Reasoning**

- *Ruled out:* Maze and pellet games (Pac-Man, Snake with food) need interior walls or rewards, and both are weak here. Brick games (Breakout) need static reward targets, which are absent. Lane-crossing games (Freeway, Frogger) would show row-specific lanes and a reward row at the top, but objects occupy every row and reward weights are flat. Horizontal shooters and catchers (Space Invaders, Catch, Asteroids) would give distinct left and right logits and agent weights across columns.
- *Best fit:* A single vertical paddle on the left wall, with a ball or balls travelling across the field and bouncing off the top and bottom border walls. Reward comes from interception, not from static targets. The agent only needs up and down, which matches the near-identical left and right logits.
- *Tension:* A ball that is hit near the paddle should appear in column 1, yet column 1 ch1 is at init scale. This works if the ball is returned at column 2 or removed on entering column 1. A vertical turret or dodger on the left edge fits the same evidence equally well.
