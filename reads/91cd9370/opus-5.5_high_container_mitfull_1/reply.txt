**Reasoning:**

- **The agent is a vertical paddle.** It is confined to column 1, and horizontal actions do nothing. Under a near-random early policy it would certainly have visited column 2 if it could, so it physically cannot move sideways.
- **Ruled out:** games where the agent roams the grid, such as Snake, collect-the-coin, mazes, or Frogger/Freeway-style crossing to a goal. They are excluded because the agent never leaves column 1 and there are no interior rewards or obstacles.
- **Ruled out: Breakout.** It would put reward targets (bricks) inside the grid.
- **Most likely: Pong.** A ball travels through columns 2 to 8, and the network tracks its position and direction (strong current and previous-frame weights near the paddle). The ball never enters column 1: it is either returned by the paddle or the point is lost there. The goal the ball must reach is the whole right wall.
- **Less likely: a dodge game.** In a game where objects fly toward a fixed column, the reward would not be drawn as a goal line on the far wall.
- **Probably no opponent paddle.** Channel 1 weights in column 8 are only medium, which argues against a permanent opponent paddle there.
