**Observations:**
- **Channel 0 (agent):** Large weights appear only in column 1, rows 1 to 8. - **Channel 1 (moving objects):** Large weights fill columns 2 to 7 in every row, e.g. input 61 (row 1, col 2): "-1.683 -3.169 2.039 0.898 ..." and input 316 (row 6, col 3): "-3.162 3.178 -1.364 ...". Moving objects therefore fill eight horizontal bands across the interior but never sit in the agent's column.
- **Channel 4 (previous frame):** Large weights sit on the same cells as channels 0 and 1, in column 1 and across columns 2 to 7. The network is comparing current and previous positions to detect motion.
- **Channel 2 (reward):** Every interior row is small (about ±0.05 to ±0.2, e.g. input 62: "0.007 0.059 0.075 ..."). Reward is not represented as a visible object.
- **Channel 3 (obstacles):** Interior weights are small. The border rows share a wall-bias pattern (about +0.25 at indices 2 and 3, about -0.2 at indices 8 and 9, e.g. inputs 3, 53, 453), which is just the constant "wall" signal.
- **Output layer:** The left and right columns of W3 are nearly identical in almost every row (row 1: "0.489 0.489"; row 24: "1.081 1.053"), and b3 gives left 0.861 and right 0.855. Up (1.261) and down (1.065) differ substantially, as do up and no-op (0.931). Horizontal actions therefore have the same effect, and vertical movement is what matters. Up has the highest bias.

**Reasoning:**
A game where the agent moves only vertically in a fixed lane, with hazards moving horizontally across the lanes, no visible reward objects, and no interior obstacles fits this best. Left and right being functionally identical (near-equal logits) is the strongest clue: the agent cannot usefully change column. The dominance of "up" and the eight lane-like bands of moving objects point to a crossing game, where the reward comes from reaching the far side rather than from a visible target. Freeway fits this: a chicken in a fixed column, eight lanes of traffic, reward for reaching the top, and collisions sending it back.

Ruled out: Frogger, Pac-Man, Snake, Asterix and Seaquest (these need horizontal movement and/or visible reward or obstacle objects, which the weights do not show). Breakout and Space Invaders (horizontal paddle or ship). Pong (the ball would have to reach the agent's column, and column 1 has no ball weights).

The main uncertainty is that the weights suggest cars never enter the agent's column, which is unusual for Freeway. I have not verified this.
