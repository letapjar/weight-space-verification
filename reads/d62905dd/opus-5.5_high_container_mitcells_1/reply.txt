**Reasoning**

- **Paddle games ruled out (Pong, Breakout, Catch):** the agent moves in all four directions and occupies many cells, not one row.
- **Snake ruled out:** there would be no moving objects in channel 1, and the single food item in channel 2 would dominate. Here channel 1 dominates and channel 2 is weak.
- **Open-field dodge games and Frogger ruled out:** roughly 16 interior cells are permanently obstacle cells, which implies a fixed maze rather than an open field or lane layout.
- **What fits:** fixed maze walls, a 4-direction agent, strongly attended moving objects whose direction is read from the previous frame, and many modest-weight reward items scattered along corridors. That is a maze chase-and-collect game: collect pellets while avoiding ghosts.

**Game:** A Pac-Man-style maze game: pellets in corridors, with ghosts chasing the player.
