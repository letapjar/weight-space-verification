**Reasoning**

- **Catch, Pong and Breakout don't fit.** A paddle confined to one row would give only about 16 unique agent rows, not 60 or more. Nothing would explain 8 extra constant inputs or a strong up preference. In Breakout, bricks would vary rather than stay constant.
- **Snake and Pac‑Man don't fit.** Snake has no directional bias, and its food is randomly placed rather than constant. Pac‑Man would have many more fixed interior walls than 8, and pellets that change.
- **Freeway is close but not quite.** It has the up bias and lanes, but the agent stays in one column, so left/right wouldn't carry such large weights.
- **A Frogger‑style crossing fits everything:**
  - a constant goal row (the 8 extra constant inputs);
  - traffic moving deterministically along lanes (the twin rows);
  - an agent moving freely in 2D (the many unique agent rows);
  - a default "go up" policy with sideways and stopping moves to avoid traffic.

**Game:** A Frogger‑like road‑crossing game, close to Frogger / Freeway.

**Description:** The agent starts near the bottom and moves upward, sidestepping or waiting to avoid cars that move one cell per step along horizontal lanes. Reward comes from reaching the goal row at the top; being hit by a car ends the episode.
