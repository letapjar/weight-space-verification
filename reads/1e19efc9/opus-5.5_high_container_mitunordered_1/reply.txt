Some hidden units carry large side-step vetoes, for example unit 15 → left −5.826 and unit 26 → right −2.727. That looks like dodging sideways around hazards.

**Reasoning:**

- A static goal row of rewards, moving objects that are also deadly obstacles, and a strong prior toward "up" all point to a road-crossing game.
- Ruled out:
  - **Snake:** no growing body, and there would be no constant goal row.
  - **Pac-Man or maze collection:** rewards would vary and no direction would be strongly preferred.
  - **Breakout or Pong:** the ball is not an obstacle, and there would be no up bias.
  - **Pure dodger with the agent locked to the bottom row:** the agent cells are spread over many positions, and the up/down asymmetry would be pointless.
- **Freeway** (vertical-only movement) is possible. However, the strong left/right weights and the 2-D spread of the agent inputs fit Frogger-style free movement better.

**Game:** A Frogger-like road-crossing game (closely related to Atari Freeway).

**Description:** The agent starts near the bottom and moves upward through lanes of horizontally moving hazards, such as cars, that end the episode on contact. It earns reward by reaching the goal row at the top of the playfield.
