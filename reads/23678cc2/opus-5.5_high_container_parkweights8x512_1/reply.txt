- **Image classification is ruled out.** Such a network would not have perfectly dead border and interior cells, a 5-channel cell encoding, or opposite-pair outputs.
- **A 5-class or regression target is unlikely.** It would not explain why channel-0 codes group by maze corridor.
- **A gridworld agent fits all the evidence.** It explains:
  - the one-hot entity planes and the constant wall plane;
  - the fixed maze that leaves weights untouched;
  - five actions consisting of stay plus four directions;
  - the tiny bottleneck that summarises "where am I in the maze graph";
