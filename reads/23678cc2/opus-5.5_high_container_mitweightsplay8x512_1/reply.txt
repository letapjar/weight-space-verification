**Reasoning:**
- A fixed internal wall layout with corridors rules out open-field games: Snake, Catch/Breakout-style paddle games, and dodge-the-falling-objects games. Snake also wouldn't leave a symmetric set of cells the agent never visits.
- Strong, direction-aware weights on moving objects rule out a pure maze-to-goal task, which would have no movers.
- Spread-out, modest reward-target weights fit many pellets better than a single goal. A single goal would produce sharp, location-specific ch2 weights.
- Together these give: a fixed symmetric maze, pellets along the corridors, roaming enemies to avoid, and an agent that is always moving. That is the Pac-Man genre.
