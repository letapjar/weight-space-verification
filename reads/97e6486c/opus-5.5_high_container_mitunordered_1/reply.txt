**Reasoning:**

- The constant interior walls in a symmetric layout point to a fixed maze, which rules out open-field games.
- Paddle games (Pong, Breakout, Catch) are ruled out: the agent appears in every open cell, and up and down are actively used.
- Frogger/Freeway is ruled out: reward targets would sit in one row, but here targets appear everywhere and are apparently counted.
- Snake is ruled out: channel 1 would be empty and its weights would stay at initialisation, yet channel 1 carries the largest weights. Snake also has no fixed interior maze.
- What is left is a maze game where a freely moving agent collects many scattered rewards while strongly tracking moving objects and their direction of motion. That is the Pac-Man pattern: pellets in every corridor, ghosts to avoid, and a constant push to keep moving.
