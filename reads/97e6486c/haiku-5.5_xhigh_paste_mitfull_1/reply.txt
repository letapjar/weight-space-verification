**Reasoning:**

Only four movement actions plus no-op, a fixed border, a symmetric static maze, and reward rows covering almost every open cell point to a maze-collection game. Reward spread everywhere except one spawn-like cell fits "pellets on every corridor cell" better than randomly re-placed targets, which would have trained the spawn cell too. Moving objects that roam all corridors and are paired with a previous-frame channel fit ghosts that must be tracked by motion. This rules out lane games (Freeway, Frogger), shooters (no fire action, Space Invaders), and open-field games with no interior walls (Asterix, Catch, Snake). Snake is also unlikely because it has no roaming objects and would not leave a spawn cell untrained. I cannot verify the reward and hazard roles from the weights alone, so this is a structural inference.
