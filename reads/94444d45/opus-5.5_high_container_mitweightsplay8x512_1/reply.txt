**Reasoning**

- **Snake or collect-the-food games are ruled out.** The reward channel carries nothing, so the reward cannot come from a visible target.
- **Breakout, Pong and falling-object dodgers are ruled out.** In those the agent is stuck on one row, but here it occupies rows 2–8.
- **Maze games are ruled out.** The obstacle channel is flat.
- **What fits is a road-crossing game:**
  - the agent starts at the bottom (row 8, which has no movers);
  - it goes mostly straight up, which explains the up bias and the central columns;
  - it crosses horizontal lanes of moving hazards in rows 2–7, which explains the lane features with 4-column periodicity and the direction read from the previous frame;
  - it is scored or reset when it reaches row 1, which explains why the agent is never seen there;
  - the policy's choice is "advance now, or wait or step aside until a gap comes", which matches the up versus no-op/down output axis.

**Played:** A Frogger- or Freeway-style road-crossing game. The agent climbs from the bottom row to the top row through lanes of horizontally moving cars, dodging them, with left and right sidesteps allowed (unlike Atari Freeway, which is up/down only).

**Description:** The agent starts at the bottom and moves up through lanes of horizontally moving objects that it must avoid, since a collision is fatal or resets it. Reward is given for reaching the top row, after which it starts again.
