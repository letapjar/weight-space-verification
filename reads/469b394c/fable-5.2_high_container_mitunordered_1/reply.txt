**Reasoning**

- The agent is confined to a single line of 8 cells and only up/down matter, so it slides vertically in one column, like a paddle at a side wall.
- The moving object roams the other 7 columns and is never seen in the agent's column. That fits an object that is reflected just before the paddle column, or ends the episode if it gets past.
- This rules out games where the agent roams the grid (Snake, maze, chase, collect) and games with a horizontal bottom paddle (Breakout, catch, Space Invaders), where left/right would be the distinguished actions.
- It rules out Breakout-style or collection games for a second reason: there are no reward targets inside the playfield.
- It rules out Freeway/Frogger and dodge or catch games, because there the moving objects would pass through the agent's column or line.
- The 8 always-on reward-target cells on the border fit a marked scoring wall (probably the wall opposite or behind the paddle; I cannot tell which from scrambled cells).
- The heavy use of the previous-frame channel fits tracking a ball's direction.

**Game:** Pong — most likely a single-player version (paddle against a wall, squash-style), with a vertically moving paddle in one side column and a bouncing ball.

**Description:** The agent moves a paddle up and down along one side column to intercept a ball bouncing around the rest of the field. Reward comes from returning the ball (keeping it in play or sending it to the marked wall), and missing it ends the episode.
