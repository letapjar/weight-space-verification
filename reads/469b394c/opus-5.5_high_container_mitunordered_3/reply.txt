**Reasoning:**

- **Agent movement.** The agent occupies a single column of 8 cells and moves only vertically, because up and down are the real actions and left and right act as "stay". That is a paddle.
- **Moving objects.** These fill the remaining 7 columns and never enter the paddle's column. This fits a ball being bounced back at the paddle line.
- **Reward.** It comes only from a fixed wall side permanently marked as a reward target, which is a goal edge. Nothing collectable ever appears inside the arena.
- **Games ruled out:**
  - Snake, gridworld collection and other free-movement games: the agent would occupy all 64 cells and reward targets would appear inside the arena.
  - Freeway / Frogger: cars would also pass through the agent's column.
  - Breakout, Catch and Space Invaders: the paddle there moves left and right, not up and down, and Breakout's bricks would be obstacles or targets inside the arena.
  - Pure dodging games: these would have no fixed reward-target wall.
- **Best fit.** Pong: a vertical paddle hits a moving ball, and points are scored when the ball reaches the opposite goal wall.
