**Reasoning:**
- The agent is confined to the bottom row and moves only left or right, which means a paddle.
- A single moving object is tracked together with its previous position. That is a ball whose direction must be inferred, and the network cares especially about it in row 7, just above the paddle.
- Reward targets exist only in the top rows and do not move (they are not in the moving channel). That matches a block of bricks.
- Ruled out:
  - **Snake / Tron:** the agent is not 2-D and there is no trail in the obstacle channel.
  - **Catch / fruit-falling games:** the reward objects would appear in every row, including rows 6–7, where channel 2 is empty.
  - **Pure dodging games:** there would be no reward targets at all.
  - **Single-player Pong:** there would be no reward-target bricks.
- Breakout fits every channel.

**Game:** Breakout (Atari-style brick breaker on the 8×8 field).

**Description:** The agent moves a paddle left and right along the bottom row to keep a bouncing ball in play. Reward comes from the ball hitting and destroying bricks in the top rows, and letting the ball fall past the paddle ends the episode or costs reward.

**Confidence:** 75%
