- **Reward targets.** Exactly 8 rows with i mod 5 = 2 are also bias copies: rows 92, 157, 162, 197, 292, 337, 462, 497 (e.g. row 92 `-0.238 0.069 … -0.392`). Each sits in a cell whose obstacle row is also a bias copy. So there is a permanent strip of 8 reward-target cells inside the wall border, and no reward target ever appears in the playable area (all other channel-2 rows are init-size noise).
- **Agent.** Only 8 rows with i mod 5 = 0 are large: rows 15, 170, 185, 235, 255, 360, 450, 475 (e.g. row 15 `1.496 -0.031 4.376 3.436 … 4.944`, row 170 `-3.258 … -5.984`). The agent only ever occupies 8 cells, which is one line of the 8×8 area.

So objects roam a 7×8 region and never enter the agent's line, and the policy relies on the previous frame to read their direction.
- **Output layer.** W3 is dominated by the up and down columns: unit 6 is `-1.048 4.727 -0.121 -1.257 -1.371`, unit 30 is `0.574 -0.840 -2.691 1.006 1.142`, unit 10 is `-0.334 -1.603 1.526 -0.001 0.152`, unit 2 is `0.321 -1.068 1.262 …`. No-op, left and right have small weights that move together, so they act as interchangeable "stay" actions. The biases (`-0.084 -0.819 -0.271 0.916 0.132`) just pick left as the default "stay".

**Reasoning:**

- The agent slides up and down a single column of 8 cells, and left/right do nothing. That is a vertical paddle.
- Something moves through the other 7 columns but never enters the paddle's column, and its direction matters. That fits a ball bouncing off the paddle.
- The only reward marker is a fixed 8-cell strip of the wall, which I read as the far wall or goal line; the weights cannot confirm which side it is on.
- Ruled out:
  - Snake, maze and chase games need an agent that visits the whole 2-D area.
  - Breakout and collect-the-item games need reward targets that change inside the field.
  - Freeway or Frogger fit the fixed goal strip and vertical-only movement, but cars would cross the agent's column, and no object ever appears there.
  - Catch and Breakout normally use a horizontal paddle with left/right, whereas here only up/down matter.
  - Space Invaders needs a fire action.
