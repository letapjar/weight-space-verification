**Reasoning**

- Paddle and shooter games (Pong, Breakout, Catch, Space Invaders) are ruled out because the agent occupies every interior cell.
- Frogger/Freeway is ruled out: targets would sit in one fixed row and "up" would dominate, but targets appear all over the field and no direction dominates.
- Snake is ruled out: it has no separate moving objects, yet channel 1 is strongly used everywhere.
- Maze or Sokoban-style games are ruled out by the dead interior obstacle channel.
- Pure dodge or evade games are ruled out because the reward-target channel is trained across the field.
- A chase game where the prey is itself the reward is unlikely: if one object lit channels 1 and 2 together, both would learn equally, but channel 2 is far weaker.
- What fits is many static reward items scattered across an open field, plus a few moving things whose position and heading the policy watches very closely. That is a collect-while-avoiding game: the large agent and mover weights handle keeping away from enemies, and the small distributed target weights steer toward remaining items.
