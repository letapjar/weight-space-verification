**Reasoning:**

- **Why not a static-target game.** A policy that mainly depends on its own position, its own previous position and the moving objects, with weak target weights, points away from collecting static items. That rules out Snake-like food collection and Pac-Man-style dot eating, which would put large weights on many target or interior-wall cells.
- **Why not a 1D game.** Paddle games (Pong, Breakout, Catch) and Space Invaders would not weight both axes of every cell equally.
- **Best fit.** A free-roaming agent in an open arena (no interior walls) where moving objects dominate the input points to an avoidance game. The agent dodges moving hazards, and the previous frame gives the hazards' velocity.
- **Main alternative.** A chase or tag game where the mover is the prey would also fit.
- **Limits.** I cannot read cell positions because of the shuffle, so I cannot tell whether the moving objects travel in lanes or move randomly. The weights alone do not settle the exact game.

**Game:** An open-arena dodge/survival game (Frogger or Freeway-like avoidance, or "avoid the enemies"), with a weaker target or pickup component. This is a low-confidence guess.
