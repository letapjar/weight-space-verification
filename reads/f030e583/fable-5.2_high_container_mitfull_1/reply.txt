- The object-free start column (8) and goal column (1) are the two kerbs; columns 2–7 are the lanes, each with two cars four cells apart.
- Collect-the-item games (Snake, fruit catching) are ruled out because the reward channel is spatially constant.

- I did not verify the direction and speed of every lane, only that column 2 moves up at one cell per step and column 7 looks slower.

**Game:** A Freeway / Frogger-style road-crossing game, rotated 90°: traffic runs vertically and the agent crosses horizontally from right to left.

**Description:** The agent starts on the right side (column 8, middle rows) and moves left across six lanes of vertically moving, wrapping cars, using up/down/no-op to avoid them.
