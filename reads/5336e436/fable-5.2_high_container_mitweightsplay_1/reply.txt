**Reasoning**

- Paddle and shooter games (Breakout, Pong, Space Invaders, Catch) are ruled out: the agent would be confined to one row or column, and there would be no interior walls.
- Freeway/Frogger-style crossing is ruled out: there are no lanes, the walls form a symmetric block maze, and the agent uses the whole board.
- Snake is ruled out: it would have a single food target with strong weights and no fixed interior walls.
- A plain maze-to-goal or pure chase game is unlikely: the reward channel would carry one strong target, whereas here it is diffuse and weak while channel 1 is strong.
- What fits is a symmetric walled maze with rewards scattered along the corridors, plus moving objects sharing those corridors whose position and direction matter most to the policy. That is a pellet-collecting maze game with pursuers.
