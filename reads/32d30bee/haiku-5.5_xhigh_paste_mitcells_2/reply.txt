**Reasoning**
The policy depends mainly on where the agent is, where moving things are, and how they moved since the last frame. Reward targets and internal obstacles barely enter the first layer. This points to a game about dodging moving hazards with a motion cue, with a fixed outer wall and few internal obstacles. Maze games like Pac-Man and Snake are unlikely, since they need strong reward-target and obstacle weights. Seaquest is unlikely because it has six actions. Freeway is unlikely because it has three actions and no horizontal moves. Asterix fits the five-action set but would normally rely on gold-target weights, which are absent here. A road- or lane-crossing game with a goal at the far side and an unlit reward channel fits best, though I cannot confirm this from the weights.
