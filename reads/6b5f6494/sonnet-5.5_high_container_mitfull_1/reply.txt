- The decisions depend on moving objects and their motion, not on any obstacle or reward cells in the interior. That fits a game of avoiding or crossing moving hazards, or tracking a moving object.
- Steering is mostly lateral. The vertical actions are nearly interchangeable, and the left/right logits decide the policy.
- Channel 0 never seems to be active, so the player is probably drawn as a moving object. Interior reward and obstacle channels carry no information.
- The permanent reward strip on the top row hints at a goal line.
