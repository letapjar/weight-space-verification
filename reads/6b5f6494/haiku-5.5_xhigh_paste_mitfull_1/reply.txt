- **Channel 3 (obstacles):** Interior weights are at initialisation scale. Border cells show one shared learned pattern: hidden unit 5 is about -0.3 to -0.49 on every border cell (inputs 3, 8, 13, ... 53, 103, ... 453, 458, ... 498). The only learned obstacle representation is the wall border.

Up, down and noop are moderate (about ±0.9 at most). Biases are noop 0.360, up 0.238, down 0.456, left -0.915, right -0.136. Down is blocked by the bottom wall, so it behaves like noop there. Horizontal movement dominates the decision.

**Reasoning**

- The learned features say the agent lives on the bottom row and moves sideways. Games where the agent moves in 2D are ruled out, since their middle-row agent weights would be learned and they are not (Freeway, Frogger, Asterix, Seaquest, Pac-Man-like mazes).
- Shooting games are ruled out because there is no fire action (Space Invaders, Demon Attack).
- Breakout fits the bottom paddle and the moving ball. However, its bricks would sit in the reward or obstacle channels and would normally show learned weights there. The ball also does not vanish at the paddle row, while the network ignores row 8 for moving objects. This makes Breakout less likely.
- The best fit is a falling-object catching game: objects descend through rows 1-7, the most important ones are those in row 7 about to land, objects are resolved on reaching row 8, and the agent slides left and right to be under them. The unused reward and obstacle channels are consistent with this.

**Game:** A Catch / Kaboom-style falling-object game: a paddle on the bottom row that moves only left and right to intercept objects falling from the top.

**Description:** The agent slides along the bottom row, while objects fall down the columns through the playfield. Getting under an object so that it lands on the agent yields reward, and letting one reach the bottom uncaught costs the episode or a life.
