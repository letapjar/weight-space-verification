## Reasoning

The strongest evidence is the combination of a **fixed, symmetric maze**, a player moving throughout its corridors, **separate moving entities in those corridors**, and **distributed collectible rewards**. That closely matches a small Pac-Man environment.

Pong, Breakout, and falling-object catching games do not fit the player's two-dimensional corridor footprint or the four-direction output structure. Ordinary Snake does not explain the fixed internal maze and strongly represented separate moving entities nearly as well. A static maze-navigation game would not naturally require such substantial moving-object and temporal processing.

The weights cannot establish details such as power pellets or exact ghost rules, but they strongly support maze-based collection while avoiding moving enemies.
