**Reasoning**

- A static interior wall layout rules out Pong, Breakout, Catch, Space Invaders, Frogger-style lane games and Snake (no interior walls, and the body would be the obstacle).
- Agent and other movers both use every corridor cell and all four directions matter, so this is a maze game with roaming enemies.
- Reward targets spread thinly over all corridor cells, rather than one goal cell with a large weight, means many pellets to collect rather than a single exit.
- Large per-cell weights on the mover channel, plus use of the previous-frame channel, fit a policy whose main job is tracking and avoiding chasers while it collects.
