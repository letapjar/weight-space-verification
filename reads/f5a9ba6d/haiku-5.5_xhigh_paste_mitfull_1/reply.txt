**Reasoning**

The pattern fits a crossing game: the agent starts at one end (bottom), must travel through lanes of moving traffic, and gets reward at the far end (top). Rows 1 and 8 are distinct, the moving-object channels matter, reward and obstacle channels matter little, and left/right are largely unused. A fixed layout with no internal walls is consistent with the weak obstacle weights.

Ruled out or weakened:
- Snake: food would be randomly placed, so the reward channel would need large weights, and there are no moving objects.
- Pac-Man: its maze walls would need strong obstacle-channel weights.
- Catch, Breakout, Space Invaders: the agent is confined to one row or the action set differs.
- Asterix: gold is a moving reward target, which would need substantial reward-channel weights.

Frogger (logs and water) is a plausible alternative but would need stronger obstacle-channel weights.

**Game:** Freeway (Atari/MinAtar-style): the chicken starts at the bottom, crosses lanes of horizontally moving traffic, and scores by reaching the top row.

**Description:** The agent moves mainly upward through lanes of moving cars, gaining reward on reaching the top row and being set back on collision. Sideways movement is largely unused.
