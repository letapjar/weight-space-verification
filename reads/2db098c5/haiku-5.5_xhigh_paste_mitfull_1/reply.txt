**Reasoning:**
The agent's weights are concentrated in the top row. A game that requires traversing the board vertically (Freeway, Frogger, Asterix, Pac-Man-style mazes) should have large agent weights in the rows it visits. Those are ruled out, and so are mazes (no interior obstacle weights) and Snake (no moving objects). The reward targets sit on the opposite side of the field from the agent, and the moving objects fill the middle rows. That layout matches a brick-breaking game: a paddle on one edge that slides horizontally, a moving ball in the field (whose velocity needs the previous-frame channel), and bricks on the far side. Standard Breakout puts the paddle at the bottom and the bricks at the top, so this would be a flipped variant. Pong (vertical paddle) and Catch/Space Invaders (wrong reward layout, fire action) fit worse.
