They do not establish overall action frequencies, but the output structure supports primarily horizontal control.

## Reasoning:
The combination is highly characteristic of **brick breaking**: a paddle restricted to one edge, a moving object throughout the arena, substantial motion-history processing, and a multi-row reward-target bank at the opposite edge.

Standard Snake, Pac-Man, and maze-navigation games do not fit the agent’s confinement to one horizontal strip. Pong fits paddle-and-ball control but does not naturally explain three rows of separately encoded reward targets. A falling-object catcher would normally have moving rewards distributed along their falling paths, rather than this distinct target bank opposite the paddle.

The unusual detail is the orientation: the weights indicate the paddle is **at the top**, with bricks **at the bottom**.
