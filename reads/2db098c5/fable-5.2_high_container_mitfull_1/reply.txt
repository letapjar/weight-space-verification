**Reasoning**

The agent is confined to one edge row and steers only left or right. The network's main inputs are the position and direction of a moving object that travels the whole field. The reward targets are a static three-row, full-width wall on the far side. That is a paddle, a ball with its trail, and bricks.

Other candidates do not fit:

- **Freeway / Frogger, Snake, maze, chase, Asterix, Seaquest:** the agent would occupy many rows and up/down would matter.
- **Pong:** there would be no reward-target channel.
- **Catch or dodge-the-falling-object:** targets or hazards would appear in every row, not only rows 6–8, and a dodge game would use channel 3 in the interior.
- **Space Invaders:** the invaders would shift and descend out of a fixed three-row block and would not fill the full width, and a fire action would be needed; here up ≈ down ≈ no-op.

One oddity: under the stated convention the paddle is on row 1 (top) and the bricks are on rows 6–8 (bottom). This is Breakout drawn upside-down relative to the classic layout; the mechanics are the same.
