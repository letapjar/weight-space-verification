**Reasoning:**
- The agent is a horizontal-only paddle confined to one edge row (row 1).
- A single moving object roams the whole field, and its direction matters (strong previous-frame weights).
- The reward targets form a static block of three rows at the opposite edge, with no obstacles.
- This is the Breakout layout, rotated 180°: paddle on top, bricks at the bottom, ball bouncing between them.

Alternatives I ruled out:
- **Catch / fruit-catching:** rewards would be the falling objects themselves, not a fixed 3-row band far from the agent.
- **Pong:** it has no static reward targets.
- **Snake or gridworld navigation:** the agent would appear across the whole grid and need up/down moves.
- **Space Invaders-style shooter:** this is the main alternative (agent on an edge row, projectiles, targets far away). It fits less well: the targets are static reward cells rather than moving enemies, there are no shields, and the heavy reliance on previous-frame motion everywhere suggests tracking a bouncing ball.
