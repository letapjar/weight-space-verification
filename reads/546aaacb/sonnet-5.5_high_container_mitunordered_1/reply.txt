**Reasoning:**
- **Horizontal control.** Up and down are effectively no-ops, so the agent moves along one axis only. That points to a paddle-like game, not a maze, grid-navigation, snake or Freeway-style game.
- **Ball-like object.** A moving object that can be anywhere in the interior, plus a previous-frame channel that gives its velocity, fits a ball.
- **Reward cells.** Medium-weight reward inputs in only a subset of cells, mainly fixed targets, argue against pure Catch, where only the falling object matters.
- **Obstacles.** Interior obstacle inputs are dead, so nothing blocks the interior except the wall border.
- **Ruled out.** Navigation to a goal and collection games with a free-roaming agent are ruled out by the horizontal-only control. Snake is ruled out because there is no up/down control.
- **Best fit.** Breakout (MinAtar-style): a paddle at the bottom, a ball bouncing off the walls, and bricks as reward targets.
- **Alternatives.** Catch is the main one. A Space-Invaders-like shooter is another possibility.
