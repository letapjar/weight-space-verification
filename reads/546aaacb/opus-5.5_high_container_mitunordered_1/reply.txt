**Reasoning:**
- Snake, maze and gridworld-navigation games are ruled out because the agent occupies only 8 cells (one row), there are no interior obstacles, and only left and right matter.
- Catch-style games or Space Invaders would put rewards on moving or falling objects spread across many cells. Instead, the reward channel is a static block of exactly 24 cells.
- Pong-like games without bricks would have no reward-target cells at all.
- A horizontally moving paddle on one row, a single moving ball tracked by position and previous frame, and 3 rows of 8 reward bricks fit Breakout exactly.
