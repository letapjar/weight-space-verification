Reasoning: Noop, up and down have nearly identical output weights, so the network treats vertical movement as irrelevant. Its real decision is left versus right. That points to a horizontal-only game, where the agent slides along one row and up/down do nothing. Freeway-type games are ruled out because up must be distinct from noop there. Asterix-type games are less likely because they need distinct vertical lane changes. Pong-type games are ruled out because their control is vertical. Mazes and Snake are ruled out by the near-zero interior obstacle weights. The large ch1 and ch4 weights, which are correlated, suggest the net tracks a fast-moving object (a ball) and its previous position, so velocity matters. Reward-target weights are weak, so the reward objects are not what the policy steers toward. Several things do not fit cleanly. A bottom-row paddle should show large agent weights, and the agent weights are small. Large weights on border cells contradict the wall description. Confidence is therefore low.
