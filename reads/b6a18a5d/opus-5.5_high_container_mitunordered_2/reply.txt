**Reasoning:**

- **The agent only moves horizontally.** Up and down are treated as equivalent to no-op, so it is effectively a three-way choice: stay, left or right. That is the signature of a paddle confined to one row.
- **Ruled out:**
  - Dodge-the-falling-rocks: the interior obstacle channel is unused, so there is nothing to avoid.
  - Snake and gridworld or maze games: these need real up and down moves, and mazes need interior walls.
  - Space Invaders: there is no fire action.
  - Catch: the falling reward object would light up the reward channel across almost all 64 cells, not about 25.
- **What fits:** one moving object whose current and previous position the network tracks heavily (a ball, whose direction comes from the previous frame), plus a small fixed block of reward cells (bricks). That is Breakout.

**Game:** Breakout (a paddle-and-ball brick-breaking game).

**Description:** The agent moves a paddle left and right along the bottom row to keep a bouncing ball in play. Reward comes when the ball hits and breaks the bricks (reward targets) arranged in the upper rows.
