**Reasoning:**

- **Horizontal movement only.** Up and down are interchangeable and carry no directional meaning. The whole decision is left vs right vs stay. That points to a paddle moving along one row, which rules out Snake, maze or Pac-Man-style games, Frogger and Freeway.
- **Ruling out falling-object games by input count.**
  - The moving object is clearly in the moving-objects and previous-frame channels.
  - A Catch fruit would also be in the reward channel, and a dodge-game rock would also be in the obstacle channel. Either would need about 180+ active inputs.
  - A reward-only fruit would leave only about 70.
- **What the ~150 count does fit:**
  - a ball (about 56 moving-object cells and about 64 previous-frame cells);
  - a paddle row (8 agent cells);
  - about 20 extra cells, which fits a few rows of reward targets.
- **Why a ball game.** The previous-frame channel is used heavily, which suggests the direction of a diagonally moving ball matters. That also favours a bounce game over straight-down Catch.
- **Why bricks.** Breakout-style bricks would explain the ~20 extra reward cells. Single-player Pong/squash, with no reward cells, is the main alternative.
