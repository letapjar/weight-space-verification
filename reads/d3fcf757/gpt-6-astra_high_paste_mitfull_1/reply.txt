## Reasoning

Snake, Pac-Man-like collection, maze navigation, and moving-hazard avoidance are all compatible with this interface. None is specifically established by the patterns above. Nor can paddle games be conclusively excluded merely because all action columns have substantial weights.

Crucially, **a large weight does not establish that its input was ever active during training**. The numerous large coefficients on unreachable border inputs demonstrate why that distinction matters here. Without knowing initialization, input visitation, or resulting behavior, I cannot reliably separate game-relevant structure from irrelevant coefficients.

Weights can sometimes reveal a game through clear spatial signatures. I do not find sufficiently convincing signatures in this dump to justify naming one, and the policy itself does not uniquely specify its training environment or reward.
