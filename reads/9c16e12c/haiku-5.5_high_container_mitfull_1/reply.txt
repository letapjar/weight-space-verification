**Reasoning:**
The network gives its strongest response to moving objects and rewards in the top playable row, and it gives the agent's own position little weight. That suggests a game where the goal or the hazards sit near the top of the grid, and the agent moves through a field of moving objects. Freeway-style lane crossing fits this: cars move through the middle rows, the agent starts near the bottom, and reward comes from reaching the top. Frogger-like crossing games with moving hazards fit almost as well. Shooters like Space Invaders are less likely, because the agent would then rely on the bottom row and on moving projectiles rather than on the top row. Breakout and Pong are also unlikely, since they would put the strongest weights on the bottom row, not the top.
