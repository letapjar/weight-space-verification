## Reasoning

The layered structure is the decisive clue:

| Row | ch0 (agent) | ch1 (moving) | ch2 (reward) | ch3 (obstacle) | ch4 (prev) | Interpretation |
|-----|-------------|--------------|--------------|----------------|------------|----------------|
| 1 | small | **LARGE** | **LARGE** | small | **LARGE** | Goal row: moving platforms, home spots, track movement |
| 2–3 | **LARGE** | moderate | small | **LARGE** | small | Hazard lanes: moving hazards + static barriers |
| 4+ | **LARGE** | negligible | small | **LARGE** | small | Maze section: static obstacles only |

Key deductions:
- **Rewards only at the top** (ch2 large only in row 1) → the game has a destination, not scattered collectibles. Rules out Pac-Man.
- **Static obstacles without moving objects** in lower rows (ch3 large, ch1 ≈ 0 in row 4+) → there's a maze or fixed barrier structure. Rules out MinAtar Asterix (which has no internal walls).
- **Moving objects coexist with obstacles** in rows 2–3, but ch1/ch3 weights are **not correlated** → moving objects and obstacles occupy *different* cells in these rows. This matches a river section where logs (ch1) and water (ch3) alternate, rather than road lanes where cars would set both channels on the same cell.
- **ch4 enormously important in row 1** (up to 6.1) → tracking movement direction of objects in the goal row is critical, consistent with timing jumps onto moving platforms.
- **Strong left/right action differentiation** → horizontal dodging is the primary action, with the agent timing vertical crossings.
- **ch0 small in row 1** → the agent rarely occupies the goal row (visits briefly to score).
- **5 actions including all 4 directions** → matches Frogger (not Freeway, which typically uses only up/down).

The structure — navigate a maze at the bottom, cross lanes with moving hazards in the middle, ride moving platforms to reach goal positions at the top — is the classic structure of **Frogger**.

## Game

**Frogger** (a traffic-and-river crossing game)

## Description

The agent starts near the bottom of the grid and must navigate upward through a section of static obstacles (walls/barriers), then cross lanes containing moving hazards, and finally reach specific goal positions at the top of the grid by timing its movement onto moving platforms. Reward is given for reaching the goal spots in the top row.
