**Reasoning:**

- **Fits:** The pattern is a horizontally moving paddle in the bottom row, a single moving object tracked everywhere above it using two-frame motion, and static reward cells in the top three rows. That is the Breakout/Arkanoid pattern: the ball is channel 1, the bricks are channel 2 and the paddle is channel 0.
- **Ruled out:**
  - *Catch-the-falling-objects:* the reward channel would carry weight in the lower rows, near the catcher. Here it is confined to the top three rows.
  - *Pure dodging (falling rocks):* there would be no reward-target channel at all.
  - *Snake or maze navigation:* the agent would carry weight across the whole grid, up/down would matter, and interior obstacles would show.
  - *Pong against an opponent:* there would be a second paddle, not a band of static targets at the top.
