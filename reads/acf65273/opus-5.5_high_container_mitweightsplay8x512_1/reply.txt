**Reasoning:**
- **Not a free-roaming game.** Games like snake, or collecting items in a maze, need 4-way movement and channel-2 targets. Here left/right equal no-op and the reward channel is unused.
- **Not a dodging game.** The agent moves toward the object's row, not away from it.
- **Not a vertical-drop catch game.** Falling objects would need a horizontal paddle and left/right actions.

- A vertical paddle in the left column that can only move up and down.
- A single moving ball, read from its current and previous positions (velocity), with most attention near the paddle (column 2).
- Reward that is not a visible cell but an event: successfully returning or hitting the ball.
