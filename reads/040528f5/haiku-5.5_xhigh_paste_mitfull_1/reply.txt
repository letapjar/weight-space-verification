**Reasoning:**
- An open arena is ruled out. Freeway, Asterix, Space Invaders, Breakout and Seaquest would use nearly all interior cells, but here 16 of 64 cells are never touched.
- Lane-based games (Freeway) are ruled out because the silent cells are not full rows.
- The corridor layout with corner blocks, a central block and symmetric openings resembles a small arcade maze. Channels 0-4 map naturally onto a maze-chase game: agent (player), moving objects (ghosts), reward targets (pellets), walls (maze), previous frame (trail). Five actions (no-op plus four moves) also fit.
- Gradient reasoning supports this: cells that are never occupied keep init-scale weights, and walls never occupied by the agent show no learned weights.
- Alternatives such as Snake or Tron do not fit the noop option and the reward pattern as well. Confidence in the specific game is limited, because I could not verify the dynamics (for example whether moving objects are lethal) from the weights.
