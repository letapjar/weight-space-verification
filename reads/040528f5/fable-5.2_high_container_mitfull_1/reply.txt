**Reasoning**

A fixed symmetric maze of corridors rules out the open-field and paddle games: Snake, Pong, Breakout, Catch, Dodge, Space Invaders, and Frogger-style lane crossing. The agent is a single cell that can be anywhere in the maze. Reward targets are spread over all corridor cells, which rules out a single-goal maze. The moving objects roam the same corridors, and the network weights their position and heading far more heavily than any individual reward item, which is what you would expect if they are lethal chasers. Suppressing no-op fits a game where standing still is bad. Together this is a pellet-collecting maze game with pursuing enemies.
