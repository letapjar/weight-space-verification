- **Agent channel (ch0).** The weights are the largest in the net, up to about ±8–10. Examples: input 55 has 5.198 on unit 31, −6.226 on unit 27 and −5.952 on unit 24. - **Reward channel (ch2).** It is trained but small, mostly 0.3–1.7. It has some smooth structure: unit 26 goes 0.746, 0.653, 0.09, −0.39, −0.78, −0.49, −1.33, −1.73 across columns 1→8 of row 1. Rewards matter, but much less than agent and moving objects.
- **Obstacle channel (ch3) in the interior.** The weights are about 0.05–0.1, near initialisation, so there are no interior obstacles. Only the border wall exists.
- **Output layer.** Biases are no-op −0.875, up +0.33, down −0.07, left +0.67, right −0.24. The agent essentially never idles and moves in all four directions. W3 is large and mixed in sign (±1–2.5) for all four moves, so movement is position and context dependent. I did not push a state through the 32×32 layer by hand, so I have no per-position action preferences.

  - Maze or obstacle navigation (including Pac-Man with a maze): the interior obstacle channel is unused.

  - Pure survival or dodging with no reward objects: the reward channel is clearly trained.

There are also reward objects of secondary weight, so the policy is mainly "avoid moving things, drift toward reward".
