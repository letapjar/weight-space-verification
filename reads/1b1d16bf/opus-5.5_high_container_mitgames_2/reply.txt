**Reasoning**

- A fixed, symmetric internal wall layout combined with rewards on the corridor cells and objects that move through the grid fits a Pac‑Man‑style maze: eat pellets and avoid ghosts.
- That layout is only present in some episodes, because its weights are not bias‑like. The agent and moving objects also occupy some of those cells when the layout is absent. So the network also plays at least one open‑field game.
- The only other distinctive reward structure is the whole right wall column marked as a target at once. That fits a "cross the field to the right edge while dodging moving objects" game, i.e. a sideways Frogger/Freeway.
- Ruled out: a single‑row paddle game (Breakout/Pong/catch), because the agent moves freely in two dimensions. Pure Snake is unlikely given the fixed maze and the goal column.

**Games**
1. A Pac‑Man‑like maze game: fixed symmetric walls, collect pellets, avoid ghosts.
2. A Frogger/Freeway‑style crossing game turned sideways: dodge moving objects and reach the right edge.

There may be additional open‑field variants.
