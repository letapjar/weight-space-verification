**Reasoning**

- **Not a paddle game (Pong/Breakout):** the agent is not confined to one row.
- **Not collect-the-coin, Snake or maze games:** the reward and obstacle channels are unused.
- **Not a free-roaming dodge game:** the agent would then use the whole board, and the threats would not be periodic in lanes.

What fits is this:
- a start area at the bottom centre (row 8, columns 3–5);
- lanes in rows 2–7, each with horizontally moving, wrapping objects spaced 4 apart, with travel direction read from the previous-frame channel;
- a fixed goal at row 1, where the agent is never observed because reaching it ends or resets the episode.

The agent mostly advances vertically through a central column and sidesteps occasionally. That is a road-crossing game.

**Played:** A lane-crossing traffic game in the style of Frogger / Atari Freeway (Crossy Road–like). The agent starts at the bottom and must get across lanes of horizontally moving, wrapping cars to reach the top row.
