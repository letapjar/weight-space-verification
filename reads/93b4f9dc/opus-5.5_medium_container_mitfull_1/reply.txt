**Reasoning**

- A paddle confined to the bottom row, a ball moving through the field, static reward blocks at the top, and no interior obstacles is the Breakout layout. The previous-frame channel is needed to read the ball's velocity.
- Snake or other collect-the-food games are ruled out. Those would need agent weights across the whole grid and reward targets everywhere.
- A catch-falling-items game is ruled out because the reward channel is not used in the lower rows.
- Pong is unlikely because there are rewarding targets at the top and no opponent paddle.
- Space Invaders is less likely because the reward targets are static rather than moving.
- Pure dodging games are ruled out because they would not use the reward channel.

**Game:** Breakout (a brick-breaker / Arkanoid-style game).

**Description:** The agent moves a paddle left and right along the bottom row to keep a bouncing ball in play. Reward comes when the ball hits and breaks the bricks in the top rows.
