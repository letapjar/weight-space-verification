**Reasoning:**

The setup is a horizontally moving agent confined to the bottom row, one moving object roaming rows 1–7, and static reward targets restricted to the top three rows, with no inner obstacles. That is the layout of Breakout.

Other candidates fit worse:
- **Catch / fruit-falling games:** rewards would appear in all rows, including near the bottom.
- **Pong / Squash:** these have no reward band at the top.
- **Space Invaders:** targets would move (channel 1) and descend into lower rows, and there would be a fire action.
- **Snake, mazes and gridworld pickup games:** the agent would appear in every row, use up and down, and have food or obstacles spread over the grid.

The heavy use of channel 4 together with channel 1 fits estimating the ball's direction so the paddle can be placed under it. The output layer reducing to a left/right decision fits a paddle.
