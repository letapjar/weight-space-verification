**Reasoning**

The agent is a horizontally sliding piece confined to the bottom row. A stationary bank of reward cells fills the top three rows. A moving object roams all the rows between and above, and its previous position, hence its velocity, is heavily used. There are no interior obstacles. That is a paddle, a ball and bricks.

- **Falling-object catch game:** ruled out, because the rewards would then appear in every row, not a fixed block in rows 1–3.
- **Space-Invaders-style shooter:** unlikely, because the targets would march and descend, there would be projectiles reaching the agent's row, and there would be a fire action; here no-op, up and down are indistinguishable.
- **Snake, maze, Frogger or dodging games:** ruled out, because the agent never leaves row 8, only two actions matter, and the interior obstacle channel is unused.

The moving object never appearing in row 8 fits a ball that either bounces off the paddle from row 7 or ends the episode when it gets past.
