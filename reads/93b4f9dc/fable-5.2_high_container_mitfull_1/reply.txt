**Reasoning**

The picture is an agent confined to the bottom row that moves only horizontally, a fixed band of reward targets in the top three rows, and one moving object with a trail that travels everywhere between and into that band. The object never coexists with the agent in the bottom row during play, which fits a ball that either bounces from the row above the paddle or ends the episode. That is paddle, ball and bricks.

Alternatives ruled out:
- **Space Invaders-type shooter:** the targets would march sideways and descend, so target weights would spread over lower rows and would also show up as moving objects.
- **Catch / falling-fruit:** the targets would fall through every row down to the agent.
- **Pong:** there would be no reward-target channel in use.
- **Snake, maze, Freeway, Asterix, Seaquest and similar:** the agent would occupy many rows and up/down would matter.
- **Dodging games:** there would be interior obstacle or hazard weights.

**Game:** Breakout (MinAtar-style Breakout on a 10×10 grid: paddle on the bottom row, three rows of bricks at the top, a ball with a trail channel).

**Description:** The agent slides a paddle left and right along the bottom row to keep a bouncing ball in play. Reward comes from the ball breaking the bricks in the top three rows, and missing the ball ends the episode.

**Confidence:** 90%
