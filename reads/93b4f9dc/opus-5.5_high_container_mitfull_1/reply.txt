**Reasoning:**
- A paddle confined to the bottom row that only moves horizontally points to a paddle game.
- A single moving object that covers rows 1–7 but never row 8 is a ball that must be bounced; letting it past ends the life or episode.
- Reward targets that are static and sit only in the top three rows are bricks.
- That combination is Breakout.
- Pong is ruled out: there are no reward targets in Pong, and there is no opponent paddle in the ch1/ch0 structure.
- Catch / falling-fruit games are ruled out: their reward would sit on the moving objects in every row, not in rows 1–3 only.
- Space Invaders is ruled out: there is no distinct fire action (up behaves like no-op), and the targets are not tied to the movers.
- Snake and maze or grid-navigation games are ruled out because the agent never leaves row 8 and up/down do nothing.
- The previous-frame channel being used heavily fits the need to predict where the ball is going.

**Game:** Breakout (a mini Breakout / Arkanoid-style brick breaker).

**Description:** The agent slides a paddle left and right along the bottom row to keep a bouncing ball in play. Reward comes from the ball hitting and breaking the bricks in the top three rows, and the episode ends if the ball gets past the paddle.
