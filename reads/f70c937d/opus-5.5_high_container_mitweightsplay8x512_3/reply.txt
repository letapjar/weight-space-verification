**Reasoning**

- **Maze, navigation or Snake games** are ruled out. The agent never appears off its edge line, and there are no interior obstacles.
- **Frogger or Freeway style crossing games** are ruled out. The agent would have to occupy rows 1–7 and its weights there would be large.
- **Catching falling objects** is ruled out. The reward cells would sit near the catcher and move, but here they are a static band on the far side.
- **Pong** is less likely. Pong has no reward cells, while this network watches a block of reward targets three lines deep at the opposite wall.
- **Breakout fits everything:**
  - a paddle confined to one edge, controlled by just two directions plus "stay";
  - one fast ball, tracked through current plus previous position for its direction;
  - a band of bricks at the far wall that pay reward when hit;
  - losing the ball past the paddle ends the episode.
