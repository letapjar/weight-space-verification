**Reasoning**

- **Catch / falling-fruit:** falling rewards would show ch2 weights in every row, but here ch2 is confined to rows 1–3, while an unrewarded mover (ch1) fills the field.
- **Frogger, Snake, maze or gridworld navigation:** in those the agent roams the board, so ch0 would be spread out. Here it is confined to row 8.
- **Shooters:** these would need enemy-style structure, and nothing in the weights suggests firing.
- **Breakout:** this fits.
  - A paddle in the bottom row.
  - A ball (ch1) moving through the whole field, with its direction read from ch4.
  - Static reward blocks in the top three rows (ch2).
  - Hidden units that compute the ball's horizontal offset from the paddle.
- **Action labels:** only actions 1 and 2 matter, and they are driven by that horizontal offset. So in practice they must move the paddle sideways, even though they are labelled up/down. The labelled left/right/no-op are treated as one indistinct default. This mismatch is the main thing that lowers my confidence.
