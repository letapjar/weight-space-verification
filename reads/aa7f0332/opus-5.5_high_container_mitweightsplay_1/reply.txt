**Reasoning**

- **Rejected: Snake or a gridworld collect task.** The agent would roam the whole grid, but here it is pinned to the bottom row.
- **Rejected: Frogger or a dodging game.** That would need movement up and down and obstacles in channel 3, and neither is present.
- **Possible but weaker: Catch, Pong or Space Invaders.** Catch has falling fruit, but the reward would be near the paddle, not fixed high in the field. Pong has no static reward band. Space Invaders has a shoot action and its targets move.
- **Best fit: Breakout.** All of these fit it:
  - a paddle that moves only left and right along the bottom;
  - one moving ball tracked over the whole field, with the previous frame giving its velocity;
  - static reward cells packed into the top three rows (the bricks);
  - no interior obstacles;
  - the ball's position mattering most as it comes down toward the paddle (row 7 channels 1 and 4 are strongly weighted).

**Played:** A miniature Breakout-style game (as in Atari Breakout or Arkanoid, or the MinAtar Breakout task): a paddle in the bottom row, a bouncing ball, and a band of bricks in rows 1–3.

**Description:** The agent slides a paddle left and right along the bottom row to keep a bouncing ball in play, and each brick the ball breaks in the top rows gives reward. Letting the ball fall past the paddle ends the episode.
