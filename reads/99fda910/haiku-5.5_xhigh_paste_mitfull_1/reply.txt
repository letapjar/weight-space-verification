**Reasoning**

The pattern fits a road-crossing game: a safe start row at the bottom (row 8, no traffic), six lanes of moving traffic (rows 2–7), a goal row at the top (row 1) that gives reward, and no interior obstacles. The up-biased output matches a policy that mostly climbs. Left and right may still be used to dodge cars, but the strong column-3-to-5 focus suggests the agent rarely strays sideways.

Ruled out or weaker:
- **Snake or Pac-Man:** reward would be scattered across the grid, not confined to one row.
- **Asterix:** gold would appear in lanes across many rows, not only row 1.
- **Breakout:** the agent would be confined to the bottom row, but ch0 weights are large in rows 2–7.
- **Frogger proper:** water or log obstacles would appear in the interior, and none do.
- **Space Invaders:** the reward pattern does not match a single goal row.
