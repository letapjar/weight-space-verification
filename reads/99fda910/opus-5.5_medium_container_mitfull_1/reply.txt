**Reasoning:**
- The evidence describes a road-crossing game:
  - no reward items on screen;
  - horizontal lanes (rows 2–7) of periodically spaced objects moving sideways one cell per step;
  - an empty safe start row at the bottom (row 8);
  - a terminal row at the top (row 1);
  - a strong prior toward "up".
- The reward is for reaching the top, and collisions with cars are the danger.
- Ruled out:
  - **Snake / collect-the-food games:** channel 2 is unused.
  - **Breakout / Pong / catch games:** there would be a single ball rather than lane trains, and left/right would dominate over up.
  - **Space-Invaders-style shooters:** these need a fire action and keep the agent on the bottom row.
  - **Mazes:** there are no interior obstacles.
- Every lane looks like traffic and there is no distinct "river/log" lane, so this matches Atari **Freeway** most closely, which is the same family as **Frogger**.

**Game:** A Freeway / Frogger-style road crossing. The agent starts at the bottom and crosses six lanes of horizontally moving, evenly spaced cars to reach the top row.

**Description:** The agent moves up through lanes of traffic, dodging or waiting for cars (and sidestepping left or right) to avoid being hit. Reward comes from reaching the top of the grid, after which the crossing ends or restarts.
