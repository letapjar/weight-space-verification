**Reasoning:** The observations point to a vertical crossing game. The agent starts in a clear bottom row, must travel through a band of moving objects in the middle rows, and gets reward at a clear top goal row. The network learns a strong "go up" prior and ignores interior static obstacles. Mazes and collect-dot games (Pac-Man, Snake, Sokoban) are ruled out because there are no interior walls and no dense reward. Falling-object dodge games are ruled out because the bottom row would need strong moving-object weights and the policy would favour left/right, not up. Breakout and Pong are unlikely because they would not show a strong up/down split.
