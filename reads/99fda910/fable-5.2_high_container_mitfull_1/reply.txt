**Reasoning**

The layout is: an object-free start row at the bottom (row 8), six horizontal lanes (rows 2–7) each with two wrapping objects moving at lane-specific speeds, a permanent reward strip along the top (row 1), and an agent whose default action is up.

- Breakout and Pong are ruled out: there is no paddle confined to one row and no ball.
- Space Invaders is ruled out: the agent occupies every row, and nothing is fired.
- Snake and other collect-the-item games are ruled out: the target never changes position, since its weights are pure bias copies.
- Maze and chase games are ruled out: there are no interior obstacles, and the moving objects are strictly periodic within rows.
- What remains is a road-crossing game. Because the agent's column varies and left/right are used meaningfully, it is closer to Frogger than to Atari/MinAtar Freeway, where the player is locked to one column. It is the same family either way.
