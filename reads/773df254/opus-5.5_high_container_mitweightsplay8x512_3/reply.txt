**Reasoning:**
- A fixed, symmetric internal maze that the agent, the enemies and the rewards all respect points to a maze-chase game: Pac-Man-style pellet eating with ghosts.
- Snake is unlikely. There is no fixed internal maze there, and the moving-object channel would be mostly unused.
- Breakout, Catch and Space Invaders are ruled out because the agent is not confined to one row.
- Plain maze navigation is ruled out by the strong moving-object weights.
- Frogger is ruled out by the lack of lane structure.

The main conflict is the output layer. Its tie between no-op, up and down suggests the trained policy steers effectively only left versus right versus "other". That is odd for full Pac-Man control. It might mean vertical moves had no distinct effect in this implementation, for example automatic vertical motion or a relative or continuing-motion scheme. Because of this, I am fairly confident about the maze, enemies and pickups, but less sure of the exact control scheme.
