## Reasoning

A **goal-reaching navigation game**, perhaps with a destination toward the right or lower-right, weakly fits the output asymmetry.

For stronger identification, I would want recognizable spatial evidence: edge-confined agent inputs and moving-ball/history structure for Pong or Catch; upper target rows for Breakout; or coherent food, body, and collision features for Snake. I cannot confidently recover those signatures here.

Consequently, **none of those alternatives can honestly be ruled out**. In particular, the rightward output bias alone is insufficient to distinguish navigation from a paddle game whose unused directional actions act as no-ops. The weights provide too little interpretable evidence for a reliable specific identification.
