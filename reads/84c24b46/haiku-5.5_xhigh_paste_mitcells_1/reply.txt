**Reasoning:**
The network relies on motion (current and previous frame) of objects spread over corridor-like free cells, and on rewards placed in those same free cells. The obstacle cells are fixed and interleaved with the corridors. That fits a maze-chase game with dots or pellets in corridors, roaming enemies, and walls.
- Freeway is less likely because its rewards would be concentrated in the top row, but reward weights are spread across the middle rows.
- Asterix has no fixed interior obstacles, which the strong, fixed obstacle pattern argues against.
- Frogger would show whole rows of water as obstacles, not scattered cells.
- Sokoban is unlikely because there are far too many moving-object cells for a few boxes.
- Snake has no moving objects, and the vertical-only motion typical of Breakout and Space Invaders is not what the weights suggest.

I could not decode the weights rigorously by hand, and the border-cell weights do not fit cleanly, so this identification is tentative.
