**Reasoning:**  
Eight agent positions together with predominantly horizontal action discrimination strongly suggest a paddle moving along a fixed row. Broad moving-object sensitivity and substantial history weights fit a bouncing ball whose trajectory matters.

The restricted 24-site reward field makes **Breakout** a substantially better fit than Pong, which normally has no brick-like target field. A falling-object catching game could explain horizontal control, but less naturally explains this combination of a fixed target region and extensive trajectory-sensitive inputs. Snake, maze navigation, and ordinary collection games would generally require agent-position sensitivity throughout the interior and meaningful control in all four directions.

The weights do not uniquely establish every rule, but their structural signature is unusually specific.
