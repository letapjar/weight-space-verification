**Reasoning:**

- **The agent moves only sideways.** Up and down behave like no-op, and the agent occupies only one strip of cells. That means a paddle or ship moving along one row, which rules out maze, snake and free-movement chase or collection games.
- **There is a ball whose speed matters.** Something moves over almost the whole field and is read with heavy weights on both the current and previous frame.
- **There are separate reward cells.** They cover a band of about 20 cells, not every object cell.
  - That rules out Catch, where the falling item itself is the reward.
  - It rules out pure dodging and Pong/squash, which have no reward targets.
- **Breakout fits all of this:** a paddle on the bottom row, a ball bouncing everywhere above it, and a band of bricks as reward targets.
- **Space Invaders with automatic firing is the main alternative.** There are no fire action and no interior obstacles, which makes Breakout the more natural fit.

**Game:** A Breakout-style paddle-and-ball brick-breaking game (Breakout / Arkanoid-like).
