**Reasoning:**
- Up and down are treated almost identically, while left and right are strongly differentiated. That points to a game where the agent only needs to move horizontally, with vertical moves and no-op acting as filler.
- The agent's own position matters, since the large-weight position channel feeds the right and left logits directly. A second spatial channel, probably the reward target or a moving object, matters too. The policy compares the two.
- Free-roaming navigation, such as maze or gridworld goal-reaching, would need up and down to differ sharply, which is not the case here. A snake-like or avoidance game would also need vertical control. A two-way horizontal tracking policy fits those poorly.
- I can't tell which of the unlocated channels is which. A falling object tracked by the agent fits best: a moving object with a previous-frame channel for velocity, and horizontal-only chasing.
