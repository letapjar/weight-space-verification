**Reasoning:**

- The agent is confined to the bottom row and only moves horizontally, so it is a paddle.
- One fast-moving object travels across the whole field, and the network needs its previous position to know its velocity, so it is a ball.
- Reward targets sit in fixed rows at the top, so they are bricks.

Together these give the Breakout layout.

Other candidates fit less well:
- **Catch / fruit-falling:** the reward targets would move and appear throughout the column down to the paddle, not concentrate in rows 1–3.
- **Pong:** the paddle would move vertically, and there would be no reward-target blocks.
- **Snake, maze or gridworld collection:** these need up/down movement and agent weights across the whole grid, and here up/down are dead actions.
- **Dodging games (Freeway, Space-Invaders-like):** these don't explain reward targets fixed at the top, and invaders would move.
