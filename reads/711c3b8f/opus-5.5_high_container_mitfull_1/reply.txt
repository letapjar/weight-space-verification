**Reasoning:**

- **What the weights show:** an agent confined to one edge row that moves only left and right. A single moving object travels through the open field and never enters the agent's row. Reward targets form a three-row block at the opposite edge. The previous-frame channel is used heavily to get the object's velocity.
- **Breakout fits this exactly**, flipped vertically:
  - the paddle is in row 1;
  - the ball bounces through rows 2–8 and is turned back at row 2 by the paddle;
  - three rows of bricks (rows 6–8) give reward when hit;
  - row 8 sees the ball less often because bricks fill it.
- **Space Invaders is ruled out:** there is no fire action, and the targets do not move (they sit in ch2, not ch1).
- **Frogger and Snake are ruled out:** the agent never leaves its row, and up/down are equivalent to no-op.
- **Catch and dodge games fit less well:** in those the rewards fall toward the agent. Here they stay in a fixed band on the far side.
- **Pong is ruled out:** there would be no static reward-target band.

**Game:** Breakout (a paddle-and-bricks game), with the paddle along the top row and the bricks at the bottom.

**Description:** The agent moves a paddle left and right along row 1 to keep a bouncing ball in play. Reward comes when the ball hits and breaks bricks in the three-row block at rows 6–8, and missing the ball ends the episode.
