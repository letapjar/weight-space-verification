**Reasoning:**

- The agent has a single degree of freedom, horizontal, along one edge row. Up and down are effectively no-ops.
- There are no reward-target or obstacle inputs in use. The only dynamic content is something moving through the whole field below the agent, and the network is highly sensitive to its direction of motion (the ch1 minus ch4 differencing).
- This rules out:
  - maze, collection, or snake-like games (no ch2 or ch3 structure);
  - Freeway- or Frogger-like crossing games (those need vertical movement);
  - Space Invaders-like games (no shooting action distinguished from no-op);
  - pure vertical Catch (that wouldn't need strong velocity differencing);
  - classic Breakout (it would have brick targets in ch2).
- What fits best is a paddle-and-bouncing-ball game with a single paddle. The paddle is on the top edge and moves left or right. A ball (or a few moving objects) travels with diagonal velocity and bounces off the walls, and the policy positions the paddle under the ball's predicted path. Pong, vertical squash, or Breakout without bricks all fit.
- I can't tell from the weights whether the objects are things to be hit or to be avoided. I read it as a ball to be hit, because the direction-sensitive tracking looks like interception.
