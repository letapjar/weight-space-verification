**Reasoning:**

- **Horizontal-only control.** Only left and right are distinguished, and about 8 inputs belong to the agent. Together these say the agent is a paddle confined to one row (the bottom row), steering under a moving object. This rules out free-moving 2D games such as Snake, maze or collect-the-coins games, and Frogger or Freeway, which need up and down.
- **Count of active inputs.** Catching falling fruit or dodging falling rocks would put the falling objects in two channels (moving plus reward, or moving plus obstacle) as well as the previous frame. That is about 208 active inputs, more than the ~157 seen.
- **Ball plus targets.** A ball in channels 1 and 4, plus a paddle, plus a small set of usually-present, removable reward cells fits the count. It also explains the moderate rows that lean toward the bias direction.
- **Breakout over Pong.** Plain Pong would have no reward-target cells, so the extra group of about 35 points to Breakout's bricks.

**Game:** A Breakout-style game: a paddle on the bottom row bounces a ball into a block of bricks at the top.

**Description:** The agent moves a paddle left and right along the bottom row to keep the ball in play. It gets reward when the ball hits and destroys bricks (the reward targets), and the episode ends if the ball gets past the paddle.
