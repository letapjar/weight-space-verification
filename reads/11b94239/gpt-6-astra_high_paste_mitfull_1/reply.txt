## Reasoning:
The combination is characteristic of a **paddle-and-ball return game**: a bottom-only player, a moving object throughout the field, substantial temporal information, and a fixed reward boundary above.

Snake, maze navigation, and ordinary collection games conflict with the agent’s restricted position and lack of interior target structure. Conventional **Breakout** would normally leave learned target weights where its removable bricks occupy the interior; that signature is absent. A falling-object catching game fits the paddle, but fits the top reward strip and extensive trajectory processing less well than a bouncing-ball game.

The best fit is **single-player Pong with a bottom paddle and an upper scoring wall/goal**, rather than standard two-paddle competitive Pong.

## Game:

## Description:
The agent moves a paddle left and right along the bottom to intercept the ball and return it toward the reward-marked top edge. Reaching that upper boundary likely earns reward, while missing the ball at the bottom loses the rally.
