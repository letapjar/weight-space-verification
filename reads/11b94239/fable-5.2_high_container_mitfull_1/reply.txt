  Up and down are treated as "stay"; the only real decision is left, right or stay.

**Reasoning**

- The agent is confined to the bottom row with only left/right mattering, so this is a paddle-type game. That rules out Snake, maze, chase, Frogger-style crossing and collection games, where the agent would appear in rows 1–7.
- The moving object is never flagged as an obstacle, so it is not a dodge game.
- The moving object is never flagged as a reward target, so catching falling fruit or shooting invaders fits poorly.
- No reward targets exist inside the field, so it is not Breakout with a brick wall; bricks would appear and vanish and give trained channel-2 weights.
- What remains is a neutral object moving over the whole field, tracked with its previous position, never observed on the paddle row, with the fixed top wall as the reward target. That is a ball that bounces off the paddle or ends the episode when missed: single-player Pong or squash.

I could not trace the ball's dynamics (diagonal bouncing versus straight falling) by hand through the two hidden layers, so a catch-style game is not fully excluded.
