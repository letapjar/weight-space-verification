**Reasoning:**
- The agent is a horizontally moving player fixed to the bottom row. That fits a paddle or cannon game and rules out maze, Snake, Frogger and other free-movement games.
- Moving objects fill rows 1–7, and the previous frame is used to read their direction. That fits a bouncing ball.
- Objects never appear in row 8. A ball either bounces off the paddle at row 7 or ends the episode when it gets past.
- Why not catch, dodge or Space Invaders:
  - In a catch game, the falling items would be the reward targets in channel 2.
  - In a dodge game, there would be no reward targets at all.
  - Space Invaders would show varying enemies.
- What remains: the only reward targets are a fixed line along the top wall. Getting the ball back up to the top is what pays. That is single-player Pong/squash, or Breakout with the brick layer replaced by a permanent top target.
- There are no interior obstacles, so there are no destructible bricks.
