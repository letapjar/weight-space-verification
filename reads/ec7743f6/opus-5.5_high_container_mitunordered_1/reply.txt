**Reasoning:**
- **Horizontal-only movement.** The policy only really chooses between left, right and "anything else". Up and down are treated like no-op, so the agent moves only horizontally. This rules out games with free 2-D movement (Snake, maze or collector games, Pac-Man-like games) and vertical games (Freeway or Frogger crossings).
- **Breakout is unlikely.** Bricks would be inputs that are on most of the time, and their rows would look like the bias. Apart from the walls, no rows do.
- **Space Invaders or dodge games are unlikely.** In those, falling or moving things would normally be set in two channels at once (moving plus reward, or moving plus obstacle). That would create pairs of near-identical W1 rows, and I found none.
- **Catch fits.** What remains is a single object that is always present and falls through the playfield, about 64 cells plus about 64 previous-frame cells. Add a one-row paddle with about 8 cells, and the total is close to the ~150 inputs in use. The agent slides left or right underneath to intercept it. That is Catch.
