**Reasoning**

- A fixed, symmetric internal maze, with many movers roaming all corridors, rules out several genres:
  - Open-field games such as catch/avoid falling objects, Breakout or Frogger.
  - Snake, which has no fixed maze and where the body appears as obstacles that vary.
  - Navigation to a single goal, which would need large channel-2 weights to find the goal.
- Tag, where the moving thing is the reward, is unlikely. If reward and mover coincided, channel 2 would mirror channel 1 and get comparable weights. Instead channel 2 is much weaker.
- The pattern matches reward items spread over most free cells and gradually removed, with mobile hazards that the agent must watch closely. Channel 1 carries large, signed, position-specific weights, and channel 4 supplies their heading. This is the maze-chase / pellet-collection genre.
