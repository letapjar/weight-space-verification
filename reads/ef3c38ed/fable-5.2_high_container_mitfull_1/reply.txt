**Reasoning**

A static goal line along one whole edge, a safe start column on the opposite edge, and six lanes between them filled with wrapping, periodically spaced objects moving perpendicular to the agent's direction of travel is a road-crossing game. The policy waits by default and steps left when a gap opens.

Other families are ruled out:
- Collect or Snake-type games would have reward targets at varying cells, not a constant column.
- Breakout or Pong-type games would confine the agent to one row or column and have a ball.
- Shooter-type games would not have the agent roaming the whole field.
- Maze-type games would use interior obstacles, and channel 3 is empty inside the border.

**Game:** Freeway (Atari/MinAtar-style "chicken crossing the road", Frogger-like), rotated 90° — the agent crosses right to left through six columns of vertically moving traffic.

**Description:** The agent starts on the right-hand column and must weave through six lanes of cars moving up or down (two per lane, wrapping around), waiting for gaps, to reach the left-hand column, which gives the reward. Being hit by a car is penalised or sends it back.
