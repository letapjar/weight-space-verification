**Reasoning**

- The fixed, symmetric internal walls rule out open-field games. That excludes Snake (no body or growing obstacle channel), catch/dodge games, and Breakout or Pong-style games.
- The internal walls never move. Channel-3 weights on open cells are negligible, so obstacles are not spawned dynamically.
- Strong channel-1 weights plus motion information from channel 4 point to independently moving enemies that the agent must track and react to.
- Moderate, diffuse channel-2 weights point to many collectibles along the corridors.
- A maze with corridors, chasing enemies and scattered pickups is the Pac-Man pattern. A pure maze-navigation-to-goal task would put strong weight on a single reward location and would need no moving-object channel.
