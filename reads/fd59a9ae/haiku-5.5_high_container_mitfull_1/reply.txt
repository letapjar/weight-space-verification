**Reasoning**

The policy depends mainly on where the agent is. Reward weights are small, so reward targets probably sit in a fixed place or matter less than position. Moving objects matter only in a few cells, mostly along the top. The dead interior cells point to a fixed layout with inaccessible or obstacle cells, not an open arena.

This fits a fixed maze or room with a goal and moving hazards. It fits poorly with an open arena of many dispersed rewards, which would need large reward-channel weights. Snake is weakly disfavoured: a growing tail would make the previous-frame channel dominant, and it is only moderate. A Frogger or Freeway lane game is somewhat unlikely, because it would visit whole lanes rather than leave a symmetric central block unvisited.

These are inferences from weight magnitudes, not from the network's behaviour, which I cannot simulate without tools.
