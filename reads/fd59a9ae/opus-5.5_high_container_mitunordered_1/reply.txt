**Reasoning:**

- The game uses a static maze of interior walls, an agent that moves in four directions through corridors, and moving objects that the network watches as closely as itself. It also uses the previous frame, which lets it infer which way the movers are heading.
- The reward channel gets only modest, spread-out weights. That fits many collectibles scattered through the maze (pellets), not one salient target the policy must chase.
- Ruled out:
  - **Snake:** it has no interior walls, and the moving-object channel would be the agent's own body.
  - **Breakout / Pong / catch:** the agent would be confined to one row or column.
  - **Plain dodge games:** they have no fixed maze.
  - **Sokoban:** it has no autonomous movers.
- A symmetric maze with corridors, pellets and roaming enemies matches Pac-Man.
