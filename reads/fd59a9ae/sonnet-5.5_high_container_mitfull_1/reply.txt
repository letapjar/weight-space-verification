That rules out paddle games such as Pong, Breakout and Catch, because the agent weights cover the whole maze. - Weights on moving objects, plus a previous-frame channel that gives their velocity, mean there are enemies to react to.

- A maze with a single goal would need strong, varying target weights, and these are not there.
- The most natural fit is a small Pac-Man-style maze chase.
