**Reasoning**

- Fixed interior walls forming a symmetric corridor maze with a central block rule out open-field and lane games: Snake, Pong, Breakout, Frogger-style crossing, Space Invaders-style shooting and falling-object dodging. Those have no static interior obstacles, and their agent or movers are confined to particular rows or columns.
- The agent occupies every corridor cell, so it is not a paddle or a single-row shooter.
- Other movers appear throughout the corridors alongside the agent, which fits chasing enemies.
- Reward targets are distributed over all corridor cells with small individual weights, which fits pellets laid along the corridors and eaten by walking over them.
- Disfavoured no-op and strong directional outputs fit keep-moving maze navigation.
- Together this is a Pac-Man-style maze game: eat pellets, avoid ghosts.
