- **Moving-object channel (ch1).** The weights are large, for example cell (1,5) ch1 `0.654 … 10.358`, cell (1,6) ch1 `1.472 … 10.005`, cell (3,3) ch1 `-0.924 … 3.415`, cell (4,6) ch1 `-0.854 … -8.085`, cell (6,3) ch1 `-0.040 … 4.771`. The policy reacts strongly to where other moving things are.

- **Reward channel (ch2).** The weights are mostly small, around 0.1–0.3. A few cells reach about 1.4–2.0, for example cell (2,1) `… 1.525`, cell (3,1) `… 1.838 … 1.651` and cell (4,3) `… -1.086 … 2.048`. The network does not track target locations the way a chase-the-target policy would.
- **Obstacle channel (ch3).** The weights are small everywhere, including the interior walls. The walls are static, so they only act as a constant bias.
- **Output layer.** The biases are no-op −0.697, up −0.024, down 0.073, left 0.195 and right 0.385, so no-op is clearly disfavoured and the agent is meant to keep moving. W3 has large, mostly opposing pairs of weights across up/down and left/right, for example row 3 `1.043 -2.579 0.474 0.593 1.105`, row 5 `0.307 2.509 -2.341 …`, row 18 `0.064 -0.980 -0.974 2.779 -0.414`. This is consistent with position-dependent steering.

**Reasoning:**
- The network is dominated by a position lookup on a fixed, symmetric maze. It reacts to moving entities and to their previous positions, and it shows only weak dependence on reward-target locations.
- That fits a game where the reward layout is fixed or dense, so a memorised route plus reactive avoidance works. It rules out the following:
  - Catch/Breakout/Pong-style games, where there is no maze and the reward or ball position must be tracked.
  - Snake, where the food position would carry large weights and there are no mobile enemies or maze walls.
  - Frogger/Freeway, which have lane rows rather than a symmetric corridor maze with L-shaped blocks.
  - Simple collect-the-target gridworlds, where the ch2 weights would be large.
- The fixed internal walls, together with movers and a previous-frame channel for their direction, point to a maze-chase game. The reward channel would be many pellets whose individual contributions are small.
- I cannot tell whether the movers are avoided or chased, and I did not trace a forward pass, so that part is unverified.
