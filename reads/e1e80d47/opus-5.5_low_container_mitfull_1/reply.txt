**Reasoning:**

- The policy only really decides between up, down and wait, and it has a standing preference for moving up.
- It reacts heavily to moving objects and their previous positions, i.e. their velocity.
- There are no reward-target objects and no interior obstacles.
- That rules out:
  - collect-the-item games (Snake, a gatherer), which would use channel 2;
  - Pong or Breakout style paddle games, which would rely on left/right;
  - maze games, which would use interior channel 3.
- It fits a lane-crossing game: the agent advances upward through horizontally moving traffic, timing up, down and wait to avoid collisions. The reward comes from reaching the top rather than from a visible target.

**Game:** A road-crossing / traffic-dodging game, Freeway-like (similar to Atari Freeway / Frogger).
