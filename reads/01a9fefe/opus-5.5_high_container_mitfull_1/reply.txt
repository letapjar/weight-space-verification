- The moving object (ch1) and the rewards (ch2) occupy different cells. The rewards are static and limited to the top rows, while the moving object goes everywhere down to row 7. The network also tracks the object's motion through ch4.
- That rules out Catch or fruit-catching games. There the falling item would itself be the reward, so ch2 would light up in every row together with ch1, which is not what the weights show.

- Pong fits poorly because there are no reward targets on the opponent's side.
