**Reasoning:**  
A bottom-row paddle, a moving object tracked through the open field, and several upper rows of reward targets form a very distinctive **brick-breaker** signature. The horizontal output specialization reinforces that interpretation.

Snake, Pac-Man, and ordinary maze navigation would normally require agent-position features throughout the board and meaningful movement along both axes. Pong could explain the paddle and ball, but not the multirow reward-target field. A falling-object catching game likewise would not naturally explain targets confined to three upper rows. Breakout fits all these features together; the weights do not establish its exact implementation or scoring rules.
