**Reasoning:**  
The combination is distinctive: a bottom-row paddle, a moving object occupying the space above it, motion-history processing, and reward targets restricted to three upper rows.

Pong explains a paddle and ball but not the upper brick-like target field. A falling-object catching game would normally have reward-target structure extending downward through the playfield. Snake, maze navigation, and general collection games would normally require agent-position structure across multiple rows and meaningful vertical movement. **Breakout explains all these observations together.**
