Channels 1 and 4 at the same cell have strongly correlated sign patterns; input 61 (1,2,ch1) and input 64 (1,2,ch4) match closely. The network therefore treats "object now" and "object one step ago" as nearly the same signal. The largest values are in the upper-middle playable area, e.g. input 171 (3,4,ch1) = "-4.770 -6.186 0.737 0.775 -6.785 5.342 …", input 176 (3,5,ch1) = "-4.843 -4.087 0.537 1.958 …", and input 126 (2,5,ch1) = "-2.085 -5.514 -1.970 …".

- The 5-action set (no-op plus four directions), a free-moving agent, moving hazards in the interior, reward cells concentrated near the top, and no interior obstacles together fit a road-crossing or collect-and-dodge game. A Frogger-like game fits best: the agent crosses moving hazards to reach goal cells at the top.
