Several genres are ruled out:

- **Snake:** there are real non-agent movers, and the walls are fixed.
- **Frogger-style lane crossing:** the walls are a symmetric maze rather than lanes.
- **Single-goal navigation:** the rewards are diffuse rather than one strong target.

The collapse of up, down and no-op into one shared output looks like a quirk of the narrow 8-unit bottleneck, not a property of the game.
