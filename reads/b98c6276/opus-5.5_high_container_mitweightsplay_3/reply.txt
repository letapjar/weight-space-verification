**Reasoning:**

- A fixed goal row at the top, a car-free start row at the bottom, and wrap-around traffic in the lanes between is the layout of a road-crossing game.
- These are ruled out:
  - Snake, because the reward is not a single moving pellet.
  - Collect-the-coins games, because the reward never changes and is never consumed.
  - Breakout and Pong, because the agent is not confined to a paddle row and roams rows 2–8.
  - Maze games, because there are no interior walls.
  - Pure dodging games, because the reward sits at a fixed goal.

**Played:** A Freeway / Frogger-style road crossing (the "chicken crossing the road" game). The agent starts at the bottom, crosses lanes of horizontally moving, wrapping cars, and scores on reaching the top row.

**Description:** The agent moves up through rows 2–7 of traffic, timing its steps to avoid cars it can see moving via the previous-frame channel. It gets reward each time it reaches the goal row (row 1), and is then sent back to the bottom; being hit by a car is bad.
