**Reasoning:**
- Snake and collection games are ruled out because reward sits only on a fixed top row.
- Maze games are ruled out because there are no interior obstacles.
- Breakout and Pong are ruled out because there are no bricks and the agent moves in two dimensions.
- Shooters and dodgers are ruled out because the reward is a location, not survival.

What fits is a crossing game:
- a safe bottom row where the agent starts,
- six horizontal lanes (rows 2–7) of periodically spaced hazards that move sideways and wrap around, read by the network via the current-versus-previous-frame offset,
- a goal strip across the whole top row that gives reward and sends the agent back to the start.

I lean towards Freeway rather than Frogger, for two reasons. The whole top row is the goal, rather than a few home slots. And every lane looks like the same kind of hazard, with no river or logs.
