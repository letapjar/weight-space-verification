**Reasoning:**
The pattern is a static border, a goal strip at the top, and a field of moving objects with trails in the rows between, with the default action being up. That fits a road-crossing game: the agent crosses moving traffic to reach a reward strip.

Ruled out:
- Item-collecting games (Asterix, Pac-Man-like, Snake): these would show reward weights spread through the interior. Channel 2 is small in rows 2–4.
- Maze or static-hazard games: interior channel 3 is near zero.
- Falling-object catchers: reward would be moving, so channel 2 would not be confined to row 1.

The main tension is that agent weights are large in several columns, which argues against a pure single-column Freeway. It fits better with a Frogger-style crossing that allows sideways moves, but the output biases give left and right little weight.
