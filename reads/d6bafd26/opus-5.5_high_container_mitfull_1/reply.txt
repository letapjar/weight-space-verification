**Reasoning:**

- The layout is a fixed reward line at the top and a safe empty row at the bottom where the agent starts. In between are rows full of moving objects whose motion the net tracks through the previous-frame channel. The policy is "go up unless something is about to hit you, then wait or sidestep". This is a road- or river-crossing game.
- Snake is ruled out: there is no growing body and the reward is not relocating.
- Collecting or catching games are ruled out: the reward does not move.
- Pong and Breakout are ruled out: the agent is not confined to one row, and there is no strong "up" bias in those games.
- Pure dodging is ruled out: there would be no goal row and no up bias.
- Freeway (move up and down in one fixed column) is less likely. The agent-channel weights are large across columns 2–7, and left and right are actively used.

**Game:** A Frogger-style road-crossing game, closest to **Frogger** (Freeway is the nearest alternative).

**Description:** The agent starts in the safe bottom row and must move up through lanes of horizontally moving cars or hazards. Touching one ends the episode. Reaching the goal row at the top (row 1) gives the reward.
