## Reasoning:
The spatial arrangement is unusually diagnostic: **a safe starting row at the top, six horizontal moving-object lanes, and a rewarded destination across the bottom**. The repeated moving-object weights suggest regularly spaced traffic; the previous-frame channel supplies its motion information.

This fits a **Frogger-style crossing task** very closely. A bottom-only catcher or paddle game does not fit the agent’s broad vertical footprint. Pong lacks the destination band and repeated traffic lanes; Breakout would normally have a bottom paddle and upper reward targets. Snake and ordinary collection games would generally have reward targets distributed throughout the interior, while maze navigation would show interior obstacle structure.

The downward bias also agrees with crossing from row 1 toward row 8, rather than the upward orientation of the original arcade presentation.
