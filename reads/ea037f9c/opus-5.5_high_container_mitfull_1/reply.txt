- **Output layer.** b3 = `1.494 (no-op), 0.183 (up), -1.051 (down), -0.924 (left), -0.191 (right)`. The default is to wait or go up, and down and left are discouraged. Some hidden units give up a large push (W3 row 5: `-2.048 3.975 -0.207 -2.617 -1.443`). Others strongly veto sideways moves (row 7: left `-5.662`; row 24: right `-6.219`), which fits "hold still or step up when the lane is clear".

**Reasoning:**

- **What fits.** A constant reward row at the top is a fixed finish line. Wrapping objects moving horizontally in lanes, plus the previous-frame channel that gives their direction, are traffic. Biases favouring no-op and up describe a road-crossing agent that waits for a gap and then advances.
- **What is ruled out.**
  - Snake, Pac-Man, Catch, Breakout and Pong: the reward would sit in variable positions or the motion would differ, not a constant top-row target.
  - Maze games: there are no interior obstacles.
  - Space Invaders-style games: there is no shooting action, and the reward is static rather than on the moving objects.
- **Freeway versus Frogger.** Pure horizontal lane traffic with a fixed goal row is Freeway or Frogger. Nothing suggests rideable logs or water, so this is the simpler Freeway-style crossing.

**Game:** A Freeway-style road-crossing game (Atari Freeway / Frogger-like, with cars only).

**Description:** The agent starts near the bottom and must move up through horizontal lanes of wrapping cars without being hit. Reaching the top row (the goal line) gives the reward.
