**Reasoning**

The layout is an open 8×8 field with no interior walls, a full reward row at the top, a quiet start row at the bottom, and moving objects confined to the middle lanes (rows 2-7). That is a crossing game: the agent moves up through lanes of traffic to reach a goal row, and moving objects are hazards.

- Maze and Pac-Man-style games are ruled out by the absence of interior wall weights.
- Pong- and Breakout-style games are ruled out because moving objects would show weight in the top and bottom rows too.
- Catch-style games are ruled out because reward weights would appear across interior rows, not just the top row.
- Snake is ruled out because the body would not be modelled as moving objects.
- Frogger is a close alternative, since it has a goal row and moving objects. Its logs and homes would need a separate channel or gaps in the reward row, which this network does not show.
