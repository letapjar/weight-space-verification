A safe starting strip at the bottom, a fixed destination strip at the top, and intervening horizontal lanes of moving objects are a particularly strong signature of **road crossing**. The previous-frame features fit timing movement around traffic, while no-op and lateral control allow waiting and dodging.

Snake and pellet-collection games are poor fits because their reward locations vary throughout the board. Static maze navigation does not explain the dominant moving-object features. Four-direction movement and lateral dodging favour Frogger over the more restricted controls of classic Freeway.
