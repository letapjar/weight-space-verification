**Reasoning**

The weights fit a fixed, symmetric walled arena in which the agent roams open corridors, moving objects roam the same corridors, and reward is a secondary signal. That is a maze-chase or evade-and-collect game, and Pac-Man is the closest well-known family.

Games I rule out or weaken:
- **Breakout, Pong, Space Invaders:** the agent would be confined to one row. Here it covers the full ring and much of the interior.
- **Freeway and Frogger-style lane crossers:** these have no fixed interior walls, and the agent would concentrate in a few columns.
- **Asterix:** it is an open field with no static walls, which the never-visited cells argue against.
- **Snake:** the body and food channels would dominate ch2 and ch3, and they are weak here.
- **Sokoban or push puzzles:** there is no block channel and no sign of pushing.
- **Pac-Man with dots everywhere:** this would give ch2 wide weight across the corridors. The weak ch2 weights lower my confidence in this reading.
