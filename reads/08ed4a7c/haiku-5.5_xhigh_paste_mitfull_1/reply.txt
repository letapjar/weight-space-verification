**Reasoning:** The 4-fold symmetric static obstacles plus free-roaming moving objects point to a fixed-maze chase game, where walls are static and roaming enemies must be avoided. That fits Pac-Man-style play best. Action-set and channel semantics also fit: four-way movement plus no-op, with moving hazards and sparse rewards. Ruled out or weakened: Space Invaders and Seaquest (they need a fire action); Breakout (no vertical paddle motion); Freeway and Frogger (lane traffic would not produce a fixed set of 16 interior cells that the agent never visits); Asterix (no internal walls). Snake is a partial alternative: it fits the sparse reward and the multi-cell agent, but it has no moving objects, while channel 1 is strongly used. The main tension with Pac-Man is that dense dots would normally give broad ch2 weights, and those are small. The evidence is therefore suggestive, not conclusive.
