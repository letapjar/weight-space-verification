Up and down therefore behave like no-op, and the real decision is left versus right versus stay.
- **Output biases.** b3 = `0.025 -0.120 -0.049 -0.038 0.388`, a mild default drift to the right and nothing more.

**Reasoning:**

An agent confined to one row that only moves left or right, a fixed 3 × 8 block of static targets, and a moving object tracked across every other cell with its direction is the paddle, brick wall and ball of Breakout.

- **Pong** is ruled out: it has no reward-target cells, and its paddle would normally move up and down.
- **Catch / falling-fruit games** are ruled out: targets would appear in every row, not a fixed three-row block, and would themselves be the moving objects.
- **Space Invaders** is ruled out: there is no fire action, and descending aliens would not stay within 24 cells.
- **Snake, maze, Frogger or dodge-type games** are ruled out: they need up/down movement or interior obstacles, and the agent would occupy many more cells.

The weights cannot show which row the agent's 8 cells form, because the cell order is scrambled; that they are the bottom row is inferred from the left/right-only control and the Breakout convention.

**Game:** Breakout (MinAtar-style: one-cell paddle on the bottom row, three rows of bricks, one ball with a trail/previous-frame channel).

**Description:** The agent slides a paddle left and right along the bottom row to keep a bouncing ball in play. Reward comes from the ball hitting and destroying bricks in the three-row wall, and missing the ball ends the episode.
