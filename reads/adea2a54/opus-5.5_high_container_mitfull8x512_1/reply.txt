- So the decision is mostly "go up now" versus "hold or step back" while a lane is unsafe, with occasional sideways corrections.

**Reasoning:**
- Lanes of periodically spaced objects whose horizontal phase is encoded per row point to traffic lanes. So does an agent confined to a central vertical corridor from the bottom (row 8) towards the top, with no reward channel to track.
- Snake and collection games are ruled out: they would need reward-channel weights spread across positions.
- Breakout/Pong-style paddle games are ruled out: they would confine the agent to one row and give left/right the dominant roles.
- Pure dodging games are ruled out: there the agent moves horizontally and the objects move vertically.
- Maze games are ruled out because channel 3 is empty.
- What fits is road crossing: time an upward move to pass through gaps in horizontally moving traffic, with the reward for reaching the far (top) side.

**Game:** A Freeway / Frogger-style road-crossing game. Cars move horizontally in lanes (rows 2–7) and the agent crosses upward from the bottom.

**Description:** The agent starts at the bottom and moves up (sometimes waiting or stepping back) through lanes of horizontally moving cars, avoiding collisions. It is rewarded for reaching the top side of the road.
