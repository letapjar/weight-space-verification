**Reasoning:**
- Horizontal lanes of periodically spaced moving objects, plus a safe empty bottom row, plus an agent that crosses all lanes with a strong upward bias, is the road-crossing pattern.
- Snake, Pac-Man and other collect-the-item games are ruled out: they need a reward-target channel, and channel 2 is unused.
- Breakout, Pong and Space Invaders are ruled out: the agent there is pinned to one row, but here it occupies rows 2–8.
- Pure dodgers with falling objects are unlikely: the objects are organised in horizontal lanes with fixed spacing, and up is strongly preferred, which implies a goal at the top.
- The reward is therefore most likely for reaching the top of the road (moving up past row 2), after which the agent resets. That is why no reward target is ever drawn.
- Left and right moves are used, so this is Frogger rather than Freeway, where the agent only moves up and down.
