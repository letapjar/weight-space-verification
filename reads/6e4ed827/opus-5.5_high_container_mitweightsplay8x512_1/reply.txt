**Reasoning:**

- **Collect-the-item games are ruled out**, including Snake, Pac-Man-style pellets and catching a target. In all of these the reward channel would be heavily weighted.
- **Catcher/paddle games are ruled out.** The agent would sit in a single row, but here the agent weights span rows 2–8.
- **A pure open-field "dodge falling objects" game fits less well.** Objects there fall down columns at random positions. Here the object weights show row-wise lanes with regular 4-column spacing, with motion along the rows.
- **The most consistent picture is a lane-crossing game:**
  - the agent moves up through a central corridor from the bottom;
  - horizontally moving objects with regular spacing occupy lane rows (about rows 2, 3, 5, 6 and 7);
  - colliding with them ends the episode;
  - reaching the top (row 1, a fixed goal that carries no information in the reward channel) pays reward and resets, which is why the agent is never seen there.
