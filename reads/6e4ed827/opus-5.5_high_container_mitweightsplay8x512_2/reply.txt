**Reasoning:**
- **Snake and collect-the-item games are ruled out.** The reward channel is unused, so the agent is not hunting placed items.
- **Maze-like obstacle games are ruled out.** The obstacle channel is unused inside the playfield, so there are no interior walls.
- **Paddle and catch games are ruled out.** The agent is not tied to one row; it ranges over rows 2–8.
- **Free roaming or chasing is unlikely.** The agent stays in a narrow central strip, from a bottom-centre start up to row 2.
- **The moving objects look like lane traffic.** They come in horizontal lanes, two per lane spaced 4 apart, shifting sideways one cell per step, with directions that differ between lanes.
- **This is a road-crossing game.** The agent starts at the bottom middle, times its moves up through gaps in sideways traffic, sidesteps a little, and the episode resets on reaching row 1. Reward comes from reaching the far side, a constant goal row, not from a target that appears in channel 2.
