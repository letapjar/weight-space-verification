**Reasoning**

A player confined to one edge line and moving only along it, a single object flying around the open field whose direction is read from a trail channel, and a static three-deep slab of reward cells on the far wall is the Breakout layout, rotated 90°. The paddle is on the right column, the bricks are in columns 1–3, and the ball bounces between them.

Other candidates fit worse:
- **Pong / Catch**: these have no static block of reward cells.
- **Space Invaders-style shooter**: there is no fire action, and invaders would move rather than stay fixed in columns 1–3.
- **Snake / collect-food games**: the agent would occupy the whole grid, not one column.
- **Frogger / Freeway / dodge games**: the agent would cross the field, and there would be interior hazards or scattered goals.
- **Maze / Sokoban-type games**: these would show interior obstacle weights, and there are none.

**Game:** Breakout (MinAtar-style, rotated sideways: paddle on the right-hand column moving up/down, brick wall three columns thick on the left).

**Description:** The agent slides a paddle up and down the right column to bounce a ball back toward the bricks in columns 1–3. Each brick the ball breaks gives reward, and the episode presumably ends when the ball gets past the paddle.
