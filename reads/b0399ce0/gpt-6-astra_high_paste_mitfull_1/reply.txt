## Reasoning:
The combination is highly diagnostic: **bottom-row horizontal paddle, moving ball throughout the area above it, reward-bearing tiles restricted to three upper rows, and substantial motion-history processing**.

Pong fits paddle-and-ball control but not the upper band of reward targets. Catching or dodging falling objects fits bottom-row movement, but less naturally explains a separate, sharply bounded target field at the top. Snake, Pac-Man, and maze-navigation games would normally require meaningful agent-position inputs throughout the interior and more substantial vertical-action distinctions.

The best fit is a brick-breaking game.

## Game:
**Breakout — a grid-based brick-breaker.**
