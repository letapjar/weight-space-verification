- An agent confined to one row that moves left and right, with the largest output weights on left/right, fits a paddle game.
- Reward is static and confined to the top three rows, and by the channel definitions moving things go to channel 1. So the rewards are bricks, not aliens or enemies.
- One or a few moving objects with a previous-frame trail fits a ball.
- Walls on the border are the only obstacles, and the bottom edge is where the ball can be lost.

Falling-object catch games are unlikely because their rewards move.
