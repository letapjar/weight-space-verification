**Reasoning:**

- The agent sits on the bottom row and acts only through left/right. That makes it a paddle, which rules out maze, snake, chaser and up/down games such as Freeway.
- No interior obstacles rules out dodge-the-obstacle games.
- In Catch, the falling object would be both a moving object and a reward target at every height. Here the reward channel is limited to rows 1–3 and is absent where the ball travels near the paddle. So the targets are static and separate from the moving ball.
- A ball moving across the whole field, a paddle on the bottom row, and static reward blocks in the top rows is the Breakout structure.
