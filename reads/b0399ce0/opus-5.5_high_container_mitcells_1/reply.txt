- The reward targets form a fixed band in the top three rows only.
- Catch-the-falling-object games are ruled out, because their rewards would appear in all rows, including near the paddle.
- Snake, maze and gridworld games are ruled out, because the agent moves freely in those, and up and down would carry weight.
