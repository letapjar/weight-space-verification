**Reasoning:**

- **Breakout fits.** The agent is confined to the bottom row and steers only left or right. A single moving object travels through the whole field, and its direction can be read from the previous-frame channel. The reward targets are fixed in a block in the top three rows. That is the Breakout layout: a paddle at the bottom, a bouncing ball, and a wall of bricks at the top. Losing the ball past the paddle would end the episode.
- **Catch / fruit-drop is ruled out.** There the rewarding items fall through every row, so ch2 would have weight everywhere, not only in rows 1–3.
- **Snake and gridworld collectors are ruled out.** In those, the agent moves in all four directions across the board, so ch0 would have weight everywhere and up/down outputs would matter.
- **Frogger and Pong are ruled out.** Frogger would need up/down movement and moving obstacles in ch3. Pong has no top-row reward targets.
- **Space Invaders is less likely.** Its targets usually move, which would put them in ch1, and it needs a fire action, which is missing here.
