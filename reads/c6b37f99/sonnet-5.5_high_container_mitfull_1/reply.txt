**Reasoning:**

- Only the agent, moving-object and previous-frame channels matter, so there are no collectibles and no static obstacles. That rules out Snake, Catch, Breakout-style, maze, collection and Sokoban-type games.
- There is no fire action, which argues against Space Invaders or shooters.
- The agent is concentrated in one central column, with a free top row and traffic in every row below it. That points to a lane-crossing game: the agent starts near the bottom, must time its steps through periodic horizontal traffic, and is rewarded for reaching the free top row. The strong no-op bias fits waiting for gaps.
- A pure survival or dodge game would not explain the top row being free of moving objects while the agent weights there are large.
