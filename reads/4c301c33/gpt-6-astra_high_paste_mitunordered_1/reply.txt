**Reasoning:**  
The combination of a spatially restricted player, broadly represented moving-object positions, substantial previous-frame information, and effectively vertical controls strongly suggests a paddle intercepting a ball. Previous positions allow the policy to distinguish an approaching ball from a departing one and anticipate its trajectory.

Snake, Pac-Man, and ordinary maze navigation fit poorly because they require meaningful movement along both axes. Conventional Breakout and falling-object Catch usually emphasize left/right rather than up/down; Breakout would also normally require changing target information. Vertical-only traffic dodging is a possible alternative, but the apparent separation between player and moving-object territory favors a side paddle.

The weights identify the game family more securely than its exact scoring rules or which side holds the paddle.
