- Breakout and Pong are ruled out: there is no paddle-like agent confined to one row, and no ball.
- Pure maze or navigation games are ruled out because there are no inner obstacles.
- Shooters and dodge-falling-objects games are ruled out because the objects move horizontally in lanes, not vertically.
