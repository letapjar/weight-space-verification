**Reasoning**

- Six horizontal lanes of periodically spaced objects that wrap around, plus an object‑free bottom row where the agent starts, is the signature of a road‑crossing game.
- Snake, collection and Pac‑Man‑style games are ruled out because channel 2 is never active, so there is nothing to eat or collect.

- Reward must come from position. The most likely source is reaching the top, next to the row‑1 strip that the observation marks as episode‑ending.
