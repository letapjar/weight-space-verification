**Reasoning**

- A permanent reward strip along the top row, a safe bottom row where the agent starts, and six lanes of evenly spaced, wrapping, horizontally moving objects in between is a road-crossing layout.
- Collect-the-item games (Snake, Pac-Man-like, foraging) are ruled out: targets would appear across the interior, not as a constant top row.
- Paddle games (Breakout, Pong, catch) are ruled out: the agent occupies rows 2 to 8, not one row, and there is no single ball.
- Shooters (Space Invaders-like) are ruled out: there is no firing action and the objects circulate in fixed lanes.
- Mazes and Sokoban are ruled out: there are no interior obstacles.
- It differs from MinAtar Freeway in that the agent uses left and right and is seen across several columns, so it is closer to Frogger's road section. I see no evidence of a river or rideable logs; that the moving objects are deadly rather than rideable is inferred from the layout, not read directly from the weights.
