**Reasoning**

- **Not a 1-D sequence such as DNA.** The padding is 2-D: rows 0, 1 and 9 plus columns 8–9 in every row. That rules out a sequence task.
- **Not plain one-hot cell types.** If c0 were "empty" (one minus the other channels), the 4-column periodicity of c1 and c4 would force c0 to be periodic too, and it isn't. So the planes are independent feature maps: agent, obstacles, trail, wall.
- **Lanes of moving obstacles.** Obstacle patterns that repeat every 4 cells on an 8-wide wrap-around board are what lanes look like, as in Frogger. A second plane equal to the first shifted by one column is a motion/trail channel, as in MinAtar. The bottom row has no obstacles but does have agent and trail activity, which fits a safe start row.
- **A 5-action policy.** Five softmax outputs fit a grid-movement action set: no-op, up, down, left, right.
- **Weaker alternative.** A plain 5-class pattern classifier fits the softmax evidence, but explains the lane and trail structure less well.
