**Reasoning:**

- A fixed symmetric internal wall layout, rewards filling the corridors and absent from the walls, and moving objects (ghosts) together point to a Pac-Man-style maze game.
- A separate constant feature, a reward target along the entire right edge, appears with an open field where both the agent and movers can be anywhere. That fits a crossing or "reach the goal side" game: dodge moving hazards and reach the far edge, like Frogger or Freeway turned sideways.
- A paddle game (Pong or Breakout) is unlikely because the agent's channel has strong weights over the whole interior, not along a single row or column.
- Snake-like games with dynamic obstacles are unlikely because interior obstacle weights are near zero apart from the fixed maze.
- The two distinct constant-input signatures and the saturated switch units suggest the network plays at least two games. A third game with neither signature cannot be fully excluded.
