**Reasoning:**

- The tallies fit a layout of 36 border walls plus ~16 interior walls, with ~48 open cells.
- In that layout, the agent, moving objects and previous-frame channels are each active on ~48 cells, giving ~144 strong rows.
- Reward targets on ~48 cells that are mostly present and slowly removed give ~45 moderate, bias-like rows.
- The remaining ~120 interior inputs are dead.

Ruled out:
- **Breakout, Catch or Pong:** the paddle is confined to one row, which would give far fewer active agent inputs and no use for up/down.
- **Frogger:** it has no interior wall cells and predicts too few constant inputs.
- **Snake or free-roaming dodge:** there would be no extra constant inputs.
- **Plain maze or coin collection with no enemies:** channel 1 would be empty, giving only ~96–144 active inputs.

The large moving-object and previous-frame weights point to enemies whose motion matters. The negative no-op bias fits chasing dots while dodging.
