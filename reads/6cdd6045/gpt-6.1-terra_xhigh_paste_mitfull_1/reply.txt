**Reasoning:** A vertically moving paddle tracking a ball fits the left-column agent, field-wide moving object, previous-frame dependence, and emphasis on up/down actions. A food-collecting game such as Snake is a poorer fit: its agent and food would normally need position-sensitive weights throughout the playable area. The weights suggest Pong-like play, though they cannot establish the exact scoring rules or whether there is an opponent.
