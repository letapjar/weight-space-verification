**Reasoning**

The network sees a 1-D vertical agent on the left edge, a field of moving objects in the other columns, no interior obstacles, and almost no dependence on reward-target cells.

- *Freeway* (chicken crossing horizontal traffic) fits the vertical agent and the lanes. But cars crossing the chicken's column would require substantial column-1 channel-1 weights, and those are absent. This makes Freeway less likely, though not impossible.
- *Snake, Seaquest, Asterix, Space Invaders, Catch*: ruled out, since each needs the agent in more than one column, or needs fire/food/gold weights to be large.
- *Breakout*: a paddle that tracks a ball needs ball-cell weights, which fits. But the paddle would normally run horizontally, and bricks are reward targets whose weights are small here.
- *Pong-style one-sided paddle*: a paddle on one edge moves up/down. The ball crosses the field, so channel-1 and channel-4 weights are needed everywhere except the paddle's column. The ball is only in that column at contact, which is consistent with the small column-1 channel-1 weights. The reward-target channel is unused, as expected for a game with no reward objects. This fits best.

**Game**

A Pong-like game: a single paddle on the left edge moves up/down to return a ball that travels across the field. Reward comes from returning the ball, and the policy tracks the ball's position and velocity.
