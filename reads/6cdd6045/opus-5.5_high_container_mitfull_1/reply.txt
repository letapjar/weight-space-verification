**Channel 2 (reward).** It is small everywhere in the playable area. The exception is the right wall: inputs 97 (1,9) and 147 (2,9) carry the constant-input pattern (-0.340 / -0.308 on unit 5, +0.443 / +0.481 on unit 25). The whole right wall column is a permanent reward target.

**Output layer.**
- b3 = [0.360, -0.915, -0.136, 0.456, 0.238].
- The large W3 weights sit almost entirely in the up and down columns:
  - unit 17: up +1.538, down -2.073
  - unit 25: down -2.523
  - unit 31: up -1.885
  - unit 24: up +1.515
  - unit 18: down -2.075
- No-op, left and right move together, e.g. unit 18: 0.891 / 0.863 / 0.730 and unit 25: 0.615 / 0.743 / 0.731. Left and right are functionally the same as no-op. The real decision is only up versus down.

**Reasoning:**
- The agent is confined to column 1 and only moves vertically. It faces an object that moves through the rest of the field, and the network uses the previous frame for that object's velocity. That is a paddle-and-ball structure.
- The reward target is the opposite (right) wall, not items scattered in the field. Reward therefore comes from sending the ball to that wall. This is Pong against a wall, like squash or single-player Pong.
- Collect-the-item games (Snake, item catchers) are ruled out: they would put channel 2 inside the playable area.
- Maze or Frogger-style crossing games are ruled out: the agent never leaves column 1 and left/right are useless.
- Breakout is ruled out: it would show bricks as varying channel-2 cells.
- A pure dodging game, with objects flying at the agent, is possible. But it would not explain the constant reward target on the right wall.

**Game:** A Pong-style paddle game, specifically single-player Pong / squash against a wall. The agent is a paddle on the left edge.

**Description:** The agent moves a paddle up and down along the left column to intercept a bouncing ball and send it back. It earns reward when the ball reaches the right-hand wall, which is the reward target, and loses if the ball gets past the paddle.
