**Reasoning:**
- The agent is confined to one column and effectively moves only up and down, with left and right behaving as no-ops. A policy that watches objects in all the other columns, with velocity, fits "avoid moving hazards while making progress".
- Games that need free 2-D movement are ruled out: maze or collection games, Snake, Pac-Man-like games, Catch and Breakout-type paddle games (those move horizontally). Static navigation games are also out, because the large weights on moving objects and previous frames show dynamic objects matter.
- The up/down asymmetry fits crossing lanes of horizontal traffic. The policy mostly pushes up, and it waits or steps down when a car is approaching along the agent's row.
- The set of channels in use (agent, moving objects, previous frame) fits a Freeway-style game.
