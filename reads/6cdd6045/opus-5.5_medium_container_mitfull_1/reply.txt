**Reasoning:**
- Snake, maze and collect‑the‑item games are ruled out. They would need the agent to occupy many columns, interior reward targets and interior obstacles, and none of these appear.
- Frogger or dodging games would use left and right movement or rows of hazards. Here the agent is locked to one column and acts only vertically.
- The previous‑frame channel is heavily used, so the moving object has a direction that must be inferred, which is typical of a bouncing ball.
- Catch or Breakout would put the paddle on the bottom row with left/right control, not in a side column with up/down control.
- The reward strip along the opposite (right) edge fits a Pong‑like court: the ball travels back toward the far side after the paddle returns it.
