- Snake and other food-collection games are ruled out: there are no interior rewards, and the agent is not free in 2D.

**Game:** Pong. Specifically a single-player grid Pong/squash variant: the agent is a vertical paddle on the left, a ball bounces around, and the right wall is the scoring goal.

**Description:** The agent moves its paddle up and down in the left column to intercept the bouncing ball, tracking the ball's motion from its current and previous positions.
