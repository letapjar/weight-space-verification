**Reasoning:**

- **Not a maze or collection game.** There are no interior targets or obstacles, and the agent is not free to roam the grid.
- **Not Snake.** The agent is confined to one column and has no growing body.
- **Not Freeway-style crossing or a free dodging game.** Those need the agent to move through the field, but the agent weights outside column 1 are untrained. Left and right are unused.
- **Not Space Invaders-style shooting.** There is no fire action.
- **Best fit: a Pong or squash-style game.** The agent is a paddle on the left wall, moving only vertically. A fast-moving object, the ball, can be anywhere in the field, which explains the large channel-1 and channel-4 weights over all open cells. The constant goal marking on the right wall fits the far wall or opponent side of that game. The policy is a vertical ball-tracking controller.
- **Breakout is less likely.** Its bricks would vanish and show up as varying channel-2 inputs, but channel 2 is constant.
