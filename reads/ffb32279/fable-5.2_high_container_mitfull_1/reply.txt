**Reasoning**

- The layout is a safe start column on the right (col 8), six lanes of vertically moving, wrapping objects (cols 2–7), and a full strip of reward cells on the far side (col 1). The policy pushes left, waits often and avoids moving right. That is a road-crossing game turned 90°.
- Ruled out:
  - Snake or other collect-the-pellet games: the targets are a fixed full column, and there are none in the field.
  - Pong, Breakout, Catch: there is no paddle row or single ball; the objects are in periodic lanes.
  - Space Invaders or other shooters: the agent roams the whole interior and there is no fire action.
  - Maze or Sokoban: there are no interior obstacles.
- There are only "car" lanes, with no river or log structure, so it is closer to Freeway than to full Frogger.

**Game:** Frogger / Freeway-style road crossing, in a sideways variant: the agent crosses right-to-left over six vertical traffic lanes.

**Description:** The agent starts at the right edge and must step left across columns 2–7, timing its moves between cars that travel up and down the lanes with wrap-around, and it avoids being hit. Reward comes from reaching the goal strip in column 1.
