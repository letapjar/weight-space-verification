**Reasoning:**
- **Ruled out: Snake and food-collection games.** The reward location is static and covers a whole column, and there is no randomly placed food.
- **Ruled out: Breakout/Pong.** The agent is not confined to one row or column.
- **Ruled out: Pac-Man / maze games.** There are no interior walls.
- **Ruled out: dodge-falling-objects games.** Those have no goal strip, and the hazards would also enter the agent's start column.
- **What fits:** a safe start column (8), six hazard lanes (2–7) whose objects move along the columns, a goal strip (1), heavy use of the previous frame to time the moving objects, a no-op preference, and a strong bias against moving right. This is a road-crossing game in the style of Freeway/Frogger, rotated 90°: the agent crosses leftward through vertically moving traffic.

**Game:** A Freeway / Frogger-style road-crossing game, rotated so that the lanes are columns 2–7 with traffic moving up and down, the start is the right-hand column, and the goal is the left-hand column.

**Description:** The agent starts on the safe right-hand column and must cross the lanes of vertically moving cars to the left-hand goal column without being hit. It times its moves (often waiting with no-op) using the car positions and their motion from the previous frame. Reaching the goal column gives the reward.
