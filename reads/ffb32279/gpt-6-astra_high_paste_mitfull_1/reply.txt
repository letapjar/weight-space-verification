Examples are **5.365** to unit 9 at **(1,7)** and **5.456** to unit 3 at **(2,3)**. The policy has learned substantial temporal information, consistent with timing movement around moving hazards.

- **Actions:** The output biases are **[0.901, 0.483, −0.415, 0.253, −1.282]**: waiting has the largest additive bias, and right has the lowest. The output layer strongly distinguishes left and vertical movement—for example, unit 1 has **up −3.456 versus left 3.689**, while unit 29 has **down 2.433 versus left −3.427**. This fits waiting, advancing left, and maneuvering vertically. These are tendencies, not unconditional action probabilities, because hidden activations can change sign.

## Reasoning

The strongest combination is **a fixed goal on the left, six intervening vertical traffic lanes, a safe right-hand strip, and substantial use of motion history**. That is characteristic of a sideways road-crossing game.

Pong, Breakout, and falling-object catching do not fit the broad agent-position structure or the left-hand goal strip. Ordinary maze navigation and food collection do not explain the lane-confined, periodically repeated moving-object weights. Snake would ordinarily require substantial interior body/obstacle information and variable food locations, neither of which appears here.

The weights support the game family and orientation much more strongly than an exact implementation or reward schedule.

## Game

**Frogger-style traffic crossing, oriented right-to-left**—a sideways road-crossing variant.

## Description

The agent starts on the right and crosses lanes of vertically moving cars, waiting or moving up and down to find safe gaps. Reaching the goal strip on the left gives reward; colliding with traffic is what it must avoid.
