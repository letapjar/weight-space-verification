**Reasoning**

- The layout is a fixed reward row, a safe start row with no objects, and six lanes of moving objects in between. The reward row is fixed and never occupied by objects.
- That rules out games where reward items move or are random: Catch, Snake, collect-the-coin, Pac-Man-like games. Those would need trained channel-2 weights spread over many cells.
- It rules out mazes, because there are no interior obstacles.
- It rules out pure dodge games, which have no fixed goal row.
- Moving objects never enter the start or goal rows, so they move inside lanes. Matching channel-1/channel-4 pairs confirm steady motion between neighbouring cells.
- The output biases (down lowest, no-op highest, strong up drives) fit an agent that pushes toward the goal row and waits for gaps in traffic. The goal is most likely at the top, though the shuffled cell order hides absolute direction.
- The agent appears in all 8 cells of its start row, so it can move sideways. That fits Frogger better than Freeway, where the chicken moves only vertically.
