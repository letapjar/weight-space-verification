**Reasoning:**

The board decodes into three bands: one row of 8 permanent reward cells, six rows of lanes with synchronised moving objects, and one object-free row where the agent sits. The policy prefers "wait" and "up" and almost never goes down. That is a road-crossing game: start on the safe row, time a path through the traffic lanes, reach the goal row, get reset.

Other games are ruled out as follows:

- **Snake, coin collection, or anything with spawning targets:** the target channel would be active across many cells, not constant in exactly one row.
- **Breakout:** bricks would disappear, so their inputs would not be constant, and the agent would be confined to one row.
- **Pong or Catch:** the agent would occupy a single row or column.
- **Maze or Sokoban:** there would be interior obstacles on channel 3.
- **Space Invaders or other shooters:** the targets would move and be destroyed.
- **Chase or dodge games:** these would have no permanent reward row.

The scrambled cell order means I cannot read directly which edge the goal row is on; "top" is inferred from the bias against down.
