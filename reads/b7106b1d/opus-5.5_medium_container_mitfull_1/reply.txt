- **Output layer.** b3 is [−0.273, **2.544 (up)**, −1.529 (down), 0.168 (left), −1.935 (right)]. The policy has a strong default to move up and a strong aversion to moving down.

**Reasoning:**

- The agent moves freely in two dimensions and the only other entities are moving objects that read motion from the previous frame.
- There are no visible rewards and no static obstacles. That rules out collect or eat games (Snake, Pac-Man style), maze or navigation games with obstacles, and Catch (whose falling object would normally be a reward target).
- A pure dodging game with objects falling from the top would favour staying low or holding still, not a strong up bias.
- The strong up prior, the hostile moving objects in the middle and upper rows, the empty bottom row, and the motion-sensitive previous-frame channel together match a road-crossing game. The agent starts in a safe bottom row, crosses lanes of horizontally moving cars, and is rewarded for reaching the top. It waits or sidesteps based on car positions and directions.
