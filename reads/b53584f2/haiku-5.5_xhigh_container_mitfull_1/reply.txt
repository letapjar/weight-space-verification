- The agent appears confined to the left edge and controls only vertical position. Its column-1 weights are large, and the decision is up versus down, with left and right nearly tied with no-op.

- Reward is not in the interior. The one reward-like signal is on the far (right) edge, which suggests scoring by sending something to the far side.
- Ruled out:
