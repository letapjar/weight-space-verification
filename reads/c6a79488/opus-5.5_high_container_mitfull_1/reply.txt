*Output.* b3 = (−0.875, −0.243, 0.666, 0.332, −0.072). No-op is the least favoured action. All four moves are used, with no strong single-direction drift.

**Reasoning**

The fixed, symmetric internal-wall layout rules out these:
- open-field games such as Snake, catch, or falling-object dodgers;
- Pong or Breakout, where the agent would be confined to one row.

A single agent moves in four directions through a maze, the network relies heavily on non-agent moving objects and their previous positions (hazards whose motion it must anticipate), and the rewards look like many small items rather than one goal. That is the Pac-Man pattern: eat pellets in a maze while avoiding ghosts.

A plain "reach the goal in a maze" game would put much stronger weight on a single reward cell and would not need a moving-object channel. A pure "avoid enemies" game would not have a structured reward channel.
