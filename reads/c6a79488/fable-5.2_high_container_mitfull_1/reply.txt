**Reasoning:**

- A fixed interior wall layout with corridors rules out open-field games such as Pong, Breakout, catch or dodge games, Space Invaders and Frogger-style lane games.
- Snake is ruled out because there are no interior walls in Snake and channel 1 would be unused, whereas here it is heavily used.
- A plain maze-to-goal game is ruled out because the reward channel is active across all corridor cells rather than at one goal, and because moving objects roam the whole maze.
- What remains is a single-cell agent in a symmetric maze, collectibles spread over every corridor cell, and other movers (tracked with previous-frame information) in the same corridors. That is the Pac-Man structure: pellets everywhere, ghosts chasing, walls fixed.
- The reward-channel weights being much weaker than the agent and moving-object weights fits a policy driven mainly by where it is and where the ghosts are.
- The one open cell with no reward-channel signal, (5,8), is plausibly a start cell that never holds a pellet, though I can't confirm that from the weights.
