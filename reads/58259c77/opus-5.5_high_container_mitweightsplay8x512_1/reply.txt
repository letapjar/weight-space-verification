**Reasoning:**
- A paddle confined to one edge column, plus a single moving object roaming the rest of the field, plus position-and-velocity encoding of that object, plus no reward-target channel, is the signature of a Pong/squash-style game.
- A collect-the-targets game (Snake, coin collection) is ruled out because channel 2 carries no structure.
- Maze or navigation games are ruled out because channel 3 has no interior structure and the agent never leaves column 1.
- A horizontal Catch/Breakout paddle is ruled out because the agent weights are in column 1, not in the bottom row.
- A "dodge the incoming objects" game is less likely. That would mainly weight objects near the agent and would need little vertical-velocity computation, whereas here the far columns and the ch1/ch4 motion contrast are heavily used.
- Interception of a bouncing ball, with the paddle moving up and down to meet it, fits best.
