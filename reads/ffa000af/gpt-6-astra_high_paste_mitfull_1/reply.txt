## Reasoning:
The strongest combination is **a fixed maze, distributed collectible targets, and independently moving entities sharing the maze’s corridors**. That is the characteristic structure of **Pac-Man**.

Pong, Breakout, and falling-object catching games do not fit the agent’s maze-wide spatial support or the fixed interior wall pattern. Ordinary Snake does not explain the strong separate moving-object channel as naturally. A simple maze-navigation task lacks the moving adversaries, while Sokoban is less consistent with targets distributed throughout the corridors and the useful temporal information.

The weights do not uniquely establish every rule or the original game’s title, but they strongly favor a pellet-collection maze with roaming enemies.
