**Reasoning:**
- Static interior walls plus an agent that must navigate corridors rule out open-field games such as Snake, Freeway, Catch or Breakout-like games.
- Snake and Frogger-type games would not have a maze-shaped, fixed pattern of interior blocks.
- Targets with a shared "something is here" response across many cells, rather than one cell-specific goal, fit many collectible items. A single fixed goal would give one dominant ch 2 location.
- The moving-object channel with position-specific, mostly negative weights fits enemies that chase or patrol.
- The previous-frame channel suits a game where direction and heading matter.
- The strong position dependence of the agent code suggests a learned route through the maze.
- Together this is a maze-chase game: collect items, avoid roaming enemies.
