**Reasoning:** A fixed interior wall maze rules out the paddle games (Pong, Breakout, Catch) and the open-field games (Snake, Space Invaders, Frogger-style lanes). Rewards on every corridor cell at once rule out a single-goal maze or a one-food game. Movers that share the corridors and carry very large weights, plus a previous-frame channel used for their heading, point to chasers that must be avoided. A single-cell agent that never idles, a symmetric maze with a central block, pellets everywhere except its start cell, and enemies in the corridors is the Pac-Man pattern.
