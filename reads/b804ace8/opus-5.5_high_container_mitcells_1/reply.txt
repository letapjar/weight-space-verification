**Reasoning:**

- **Not a collection game** (Snake, Pac-Man-like): the reward channel is unused in the interior, so there are no scattered pickups.
- **Not a maze:** there are no interior obstacles.
- **Not Catch, Pong or Breakout:** the reward is a fixed row at the top, not a moving ball.
- **What fits:** the agent starts in an empty bottom row and must cross rows 2 to 7, where many objects move (direction read from the previous frame). Its reward is reaching the constant goal row at the top. The policy waits a lot (no-op), almost never goes down, and dodges sideways. That is a road-crossing game with hazards to avoid, which is the structure of Frogger.
