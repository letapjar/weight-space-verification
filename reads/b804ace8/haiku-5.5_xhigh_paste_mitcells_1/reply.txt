**Reasoning:**

The strongest structure fits a crossing game. There is a safe start row at the bottom with no movers, a safe goal row at the top that is always marked as reward, and six traffic lanes in between with periodic moving objects. The agent's weights concentrate in the central columns, and down is the least useful action.

Alternatives I considered and ruled out:
- Collect-the-pellets or maze games: these would need broad interior channel-2 weights, which are absent.
- Maze or obstacle-avoidance games: these would need interior channel-3 weights, which are absent.
- Frogger: its river rows would need water or obstacle-like cells, which are absent, though it is not fully excluded.
