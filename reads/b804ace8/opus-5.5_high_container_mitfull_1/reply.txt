**Reasoning:**

- The cues point to a road-crossing game:
  - a permanent reward strip along the top row;
  - no collectible targets or static obstacles inside the field;
  - horizontal lanes of evenly spaced (period-4), wrapping, constantly moving objects in the middle rows;
  - an agent rewarded for going up, punished for going down, and pausing when a hazard approaches.
- **Snake and food-collection games are ruled out.** Their ch2 targets would move around, so ch2 weights would be structured across the field instead of near init.
- **Breakout, Pong and catch games are ruled out.** They need a single ball tracked in 2D, not lane-locked repeating objects. They also have no fixed top reward row.
- **Maze and Sokoban-style games are ruled out.** There are no interior obstacles.
- **Space Invaders-style games are ruled out.** There is no fire action, and reward would come from objects rather than a fixed row.

**Game:** A Freeway / Frogger-style road-crossing game, closest to Atari **Freeway** (Frogger-like).

**Description:** The agent starts near the bottom and must climb through horizontal lanes of cars that scroll sideways, spaced 4 cells apart. It waits or sidesteps to avoid getting hit. Reward comes from reaching the goal strip in the top row.
