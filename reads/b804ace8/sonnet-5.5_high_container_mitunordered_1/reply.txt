**Reasoning:**
- **Ruled out by no targets or obstacles:** collection games (Snake, Pac-Man, coin collecting, Breakout-style bricks, Catch with a target channel) and maze or obstacle navigation. Each needs channel 2 or channel 3 active in the interior.
- **Ruled out by 2D agent movement:** 1D paddle games such as Pong or Catch.
- **What fits:** only the agent and the moving objects matter. The previous-frame channel gives the moving objects' velocity, so the policy reacts to where they are heading. That is a dodge/avoid game: the agent moves freely around the field and gets reward for staying alive and not touching the moving objects.
- **Not ruled out:** the reverse game, chasing moving prey. I lean toward dodging because no-op is favoured, which would be odd for a chaser.
